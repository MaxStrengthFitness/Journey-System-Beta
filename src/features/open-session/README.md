# The open session

A session started before the client is chosen: the Client Directory's **Open session** button. It is stored as an ordinary session with `isUnassigned: true` and no `clientId`. The round is `docs/rounds/2026-10-09-open-session.md` (AJ's picks "1b 2a 3a"); read it first.

The Hub peek's resume button is a different thing, and since Oct 10 2026 it says **Back to the session** (AJ: "yes" to taking "Open session" off it), so the two no longer share a name.

## Start (`start.ts`, used by `src/hooks/useClientMutations.ts`)

- **One write, never awaited.** The id is made on the iPad (`doc(collection(db, "sessions"))`), one `setDoc` is issued, and the screen moves to the Active Session in the same tap, online or off. A refusal comes back through the write's promise: a toast (`OPEN_SESSION_REFUSED`), and the device forgets the session.
- **Nothing is seeded.** The session records an empty list (`sessionMachineIds: []`) and no sets. It used to seed six machines by Title Case names the floor spells in capitals, and where one matched it wrote a ghost weight "0".
- **Signed as a client Start signs it** (`openSessionPayload`): `trainerId`, `trainerName`, `trainerInitials` and `startedByTrainerId` as a client Start writes them (the trainer document's id, which every reader of `startedByTrainerId` compares; the sign-in uid only when the trainer document has no id), `clientStartTime` from the iPad's clock (the timer runs from it until the server's start time arrives), `lastHeartbeatAt`, `pausedAt: null` and `totalPausedMs: 0`.
- **At this iPad's studio only.** With no studio on the iPad it writes nothing and says "Choose a studio first": the Active Session finds an open session in the studio on screen, so one written elsewhere could not be found again.
- **One open session a trainer.** While the trainer's own open session is running, Open session goes back to it and writes nothing (`runningOpenSessionId`): their newest live open session in the studio's sessions stream, whatever else of theirs runs (a client's session with a newer heartbeat used to hide it); the one this iPad started last AT THIS STUDIO, while the device remembers it, before the database has it (offline; one started at another studio is not followed after a studio switch, since the screen here can't find it); after a reload, the device's remembered session as the iPad's own copy holds it (`getDocFromCache`, no network: offline, a session just started is in no stream, since the stream ranges over the server's `createdAt`); then their abandoned one (below). Once it is finished, assigned, discarded, refused or taken over, the next tap starts a new one. The Directory's button says "Back to the open session" while one runs, and "Starting…" for a moment after a Start; a second tap inside `OPEN_SESSION_GUARD_MS` at the same studio opens the session just started even on an iPad that cannot remember one.
- **An abandoned open session is asked about** (the whole-branch review, Oct 9 2026). Left an hour with nothing typed, it could not be reached from anywhere, Who's this? with it, and a second one was started. Open session now goes back to the trainer's newest abandoned one, and the Active Session asks over its empty screen (`features/tracker/StaleSessionDialog.tsx`, no client named): **Resume it** (a heartbeat, the device points at it, Who's this? still there) or **Start a new session** (it is left exactly as it is, `declineStaleOpenSession` remembers that for this visit, and a new open session starts through the tracker's `onStartOpenSession`). `pickOpenSession`'s `stale` is that session.
- **A refused Start** (`announceOpenSessionRefused`): the Active Session holding it, or sets typed into it, says so itself and sends what is still waiting: "The sets typed are kept: tell a leader" (`OPEN_SESSION_REFUSED_KEPT`), as a client Start's refusal does. With no screen to say it, the Start's own toast does (`OPEN_SESSION_REFUSED`).
- **The device remembers it** (`rememberLiveSession`).

## The way back (`src/lib/live-session.ts`)

- `findMyLiveSession` counts this trainer's live open session, so the Session tab appears, labelled "Open session" on the iPad.
- `resumeSession` is the tab, wired (AppContent hands it the reads and the moves): `resumeTarget`, a client's session or the open session (no client chosen, the device pointed at it), else `rememberedTarget`, the session the device remembered, read by its id, while it is live. An open session at another studio is said ("Your open session is at Solon. Switch to Solon to go back to it.") and kept, never followed to a screen that can't find it.
- `pickOpenSession` is which of the studio's open sessions this iPad records: a take-over still settling, the one on screen (however long its pause), the one the device remembers while it is live, then the newest live one of this trainer's. An abandoned one is never taken up without asking (Sep 24 2026), nor watched: a refused Start, or an Assign with an old one left over, used to put the sets typed next into yesterday's session; it comes back as `stale`, for the question above. It took the first of this trainer's it came to, so a second open session could bring back the old one.
- Sign-out's question and the new-version line call it "your open session" (`ownSessionName` gives them ""), never "your session with a client".

## On the Active Session

- The open-session listener follows a studio switch (the studio is one of its dependencies), and reads the newest twenty (`orderBy("createdAt", "desc")`, served by its own composite: `hostedAtStudioId`, `isUnassigned`, `status`, `createdAt` descending, in `firestore.indexes.json`, deployed before the app; the review, Oct 9 2026): unordered, a session just started could fall outside twenty abandoned ones.
- Until the studio's open sessions answer, the screen says "Opening the session…" (`nothingKind`'s `opening`), never "No session is open here"; a read of them that FAILED says "Couldn't read this studio's open sessions." with Try again, which opens the listener again (`open-failed`): a failed read is unknown, never "none".
- **A client's screen let go of.** From a client's briefing, the Session tab to this trainer's open session keeps the Active Session mounted with no client; the last client is let go of then (their name was on the open session's bar), and the settings the screen reads are always the client on screen's own (`settingsHeld`, kept with whose they are): a client chosen a moment before Finish got the last client's seat and positions written onto theirs.
- Notes and Pulse are not drawn while there is no client: both need one, and they were buttons that opened nothing.

## The FileMaker floor (AJ's "1b")

- **Every machine shows, in the studio's walking order.** An open session (and a client session with no routine, so every routine-less session looks alike) opens the grid's fold: every row has the Today column's +. On a phone, today's cards and then the rest of the floor as names, each with an Add. For a client, the floor opens once the client's routines are known and a Start that could not decide its routine has (a routine may still come; it never opens for a frame and folds again). The groups and the corner say "Today" and "Rest of the floor", never "routine".
- **+ adds and focuses in one tap**, and the machine rises into today's numbered group in the order done (FileMaker's circle). The empty Now Bar says "Tap + on a machine you're doing." and keeps Add a machine. The order line speaks from two machines, as for every session, and also when every machine on the floor was added: the trainer chose that order.
- **The + is 40px to tap on a floor of any size**: while a + is on screen the grid never fits a row under 40px, and scrolls instead (`JourneyGrid`'s `rowFloor`).
- **One tap, one machine.** A second add of another machine inside 400ms is the first tap landing on the row that slid under it, and is let go (`journey-grid/add-bounce.ts`).
- **Out of service** on the studio's roster: the machine is on the floor, says "Out of service" in its Today cell (and on a phone's list), and has no +. Add a machine still reaches it if the mark is stale.
- **The floor is a view.** The session records only what was added (`sessionMachineIds`), never the floor. A session with its own list is never read as Free (`routine-plan/next-time.ts`, `ranAsFree` and `ranWholeFloorUnchosen`): a client session with no routine gets Next time from what was added. An open session gets there once Who's this? gives it a client: its Finish is then the ordinary one (below). An old Free session (no routine, no list of its own, the whole floor) is as it was.
- **Start from a routine…** (the builder on demand): the grid's corner (and the phone's foot) opens a sheet of routines to lay on today's list in one tap: the client's Routine A and B once Who's this? has given the session a client, the studio's starting routines (day one, the Academy's eleven before the seed), the studio's templates (its trainers' saved ones too, as the Edit routine drawer lists them) and head office's, each by its name and its first machines. The machines go on at the top of today's list in the routine's order, what is already done kept first, and the rest of the floor stays below. Today's list only: nothing writes a routine, and Routine A still comes only through the Wrap-up's Next time (`routine-plan/start-from.ts`, `routine-plan/ui/StartFromRoutineSheet.tsx`). Laying the client's own Routine A or B on a session with no routine means the session ran it: the tracker keeps which (`laidRoutineRef`, never written on the session) and Finish's Next time offers today's machines to that routine (the whole-branch review, Oct 9 2026; a Routine A still being built never heard what was done).
- The rules are in `features/journey-grid/README.md` §2.6 and `features/phone/README.md`.

## Who's this? (`assign.ts`; finding 3)

- **Any time, from the session bar.** An open session's bar says "Open session" and has **Who's this?** (40px, the raised button in the live blue, its words always shown) in place of Notes and Pulse. It opens the client picker (`features/tracker/ClientSelectionDialog.tsx`). Choosing someone gives the session to them and it **carries on as theirs**, on screen at once: the same session, its sets and its list, the floor still open (`assignedHereId`), and the client's settings, history, totals, journal and plan arrive as their listeners answer (the tracker's `clientId` becomes the client's). Nothing restarts and nothing is finished.
- **Still at Finish.** The End session question of an open session asks "Who is this session for?" with Assign to client, Create new client and Delete session. Once the client is chosen, the question comes back as theirs (the Note for the next trainer, the started-no-count answers, Finish session), and its Finish is the ordinary one: `commitEndSession`, the session number, the counters, the machine totals, the Wrap-up and **Next time**. It used to skip Finish, mark the session Completed and open the profile.
- **One batch, never awaited** (`assignSessionPatch`, `setsToAssign`, `assignSetPatch`). The session gets the client's fields as a client Start writes them (`clientId`, `isUnassigned: false`, `clientName`, `mindbodyClientId`, `homeStudioId` and `clientHomeStudioId`, `isCrossTrain` from where it is run, `sessionNumber` the client's count + 1, a heartbeat); the client's snapshot for analytics (`clientAge` ...) is Finish's, as for every session. Every set of the session gets the client, merged so its numbers are untouched: the sets on screen, the ones on the typing timer (sent first, so they are on record before the batch names them), the iPad's own copy of the database (`getDocsFromCache`, at once, offline too) and, when that copy may not hold them all (the sets listener has not had the server's answer: a session just taken over or resumed), the server's list too (`getDocs`, online, for `ASSIGN_SERVER_READ_WAIT_MS` at most). The sets are gathered when the batch is built, never at the tap, so a set typed while the reads were out is in it: the rules never let a set follow on its own once the session has its client, and Finish would then be refused for it every time. A Finish tapped while the reads are out issues the batch at once with what is known, ahead of its own (`assignIssueRef`), and never waits. A set that already has THIS client is named again; one with another client is left alone. It used to await each step and write the sets one at a time.
- **Her first Journey day.** A client whose first session this is gets `firstSessionDate` in its own write, as a client Start writes it (`startClientPatch`): "Client since", milestones and Month's anniversaries read it, and nothing else writes it. Only once the batch has COMMITTED (the whole-branch review, Oct 9 2026): a refused assign marked a first day for a session that never went onto their record.
- **Discard while the reads are out** drops the assign (`assignDroppedRef`): it is never issued after the Discard, and nothing points the device at the deleted session or switches the client on screen. A batch built once the trainer has left the screen still goes (the client was chosen), but the screen they are on now is never switched to the client.
- **Finish straight after Who's this?** The client's settings listener has only just opened, so a machine missing from the settings is not "nothing on file" until the server has answered them (`settingsServer`, heard through metadata changes, as the routines are): until then Finish writes today's weight only, never `settings: {}` (a merge would still wipe the saved seat and positions) and never a starting weight over the one on file (`completeWorkoutSession`'s `settingsOnFileKnown`). The same holds for a client session whose settings the iPad's cache answered empty. Only the server's answer says what is on file (the whole-branch review, Oct 9 2026): an entry on the iPad may be a partial document a merge left (the assign's held set-up, the card's dials-only save), so no starting weight is written off it. And the starting weight is today's only on the client's FIRST performed set on the machine (their totals answered and hold no earlier one): a Finish that wrote none used to have the next Finish backfill the second session's weight as the start; with none on file the figure falls back to the first counted set, which is right.
- **Finish straight after Who's this?, before the client's routines answer**: Next time's inputs are frozen at Finish and the card is worked out on the Wrap-up the moment the routines are known (`nextTimeLater`), never dropped and never guessed. And the Wrap-up's lines compare nothing and call nothing a first until the client's earlier sessions and their sets are the server's (`priorKnown`): the grid held only this session.
- **A client added here** (New client) has nothing on file anywhere: their empty routines are the answer even from the iPad's copy (offline included), every machine is a first time (`completeWorkoutSession`'s `newClient`), and what is read of their history is all there is (`createdHereRef`).
- **The rules** (`firestore.rules`, `logTakesItsSessionsClient`): a set's client never changes, except that a set with NO client may take the client its own session is given in the same write, while that session is still open, and that write must also CLOSE it (`isUnassigned: false` after it; a session left open with a client let any set of it take that client on its own). Finish closes a session that was ever open too (`completeWorkoutSession`), so a Finish that lands after a refused Who's this? is that write. They refused the old Assign's sets, so the session moved and its sets didn't, with only a `console.error` and a blank screen. Since the whole-branch review (Oct 9 2026), also: a set of a still-open session may let go of a client (`logLeavesItsOpenSessionsClient`), and an open session is given only a client on record once the write lands, and keeps `heldSetup` only while it is open (`openSessionWriteOk`).
- **A refusal** is a toast (`assignRefusedWords`), and the session is open again on screen, with Who's this?, never a blank one. The sets on screen are sent again LETTING GO of the client (`resendSetFields` with `clientId: deleteField()`; the rules allow it while the session is open): a set typed after the tap was written naming the client, and one that didn't exist yet landed with it although the batch was refused, so choosing a different client next would have left it behind and Finish would have been refused for it every time. The client on screen goes back to none through AppContent's raw setter (`setSelectedClientIdNow`, which Who's this? itself uses once it has asked at the tap), so a late refusal never pops a leave question nobody asked for. After a Finish, it says nothing (Finish's own batch names the client and speaks for itself); after the trainer has left the screen, it is said and changes nothing on the screen they are on. While the batch is on its way the open sessions' listener never takes the session off the screen as it leaves that list (`assigningRef`), and a second tap in the closing picker names nobody else.
- **New client** (in the picker, and New client at Finish) is the Add a client form drawn over the session (`CreateClientModal`, portalled to the page) instead of in place of the whole screen: the new person is written, the session is given to them (`onClientCreated` hands back the client as written, so the screen has them before the studio's client list does), and it carries on, or the question comes back as theirs at Finish. It used to unmount the session and leave it In-Progress with nobody. Its Save never waits on the network: the id is made on the iPad and the write issued, so the session is given to them in the same tap, offline too, and a refusal is a toast. Its typing is registered with `useUnsavedChanges` and let go as it saves, so handing the client on never asks about the form it saves. Its own write is issued before the assign's batch, and when it is refused (say, a home studio this trainer isn't on) the batch is refused with it (the rules: an open session is given only a client on record), so no session or set ever names a client that doesn't exist. The picker says "Reading the studio's clients…" or "Couldn't read the studio's clients just now." while the roster hasn't answered (`clientsStatus`), never "No clients found".
- **The settings held on the session go with it** (below): in the same batch, and `heldSetup` is deleted in the session's write.
- **The picker** finds a person the way the Client Directory does (`client-directory/search.ts`: nicknames both ways, punctuation, part of a name, close matches said as such), lists names only (it showed each client's height and body weight), wraps every name, and offers New client.

## Settings held on the session (`held-setup.ts`, `held-store.ts`; finding 4, AJ's "3a")

- **Before the client is chosen, the machine card keeps its settings on the session**: `sessions/{id}.heldSetup.{machineId}` = `{ values, sources?, at, byUid }` (`sources` only for a value taken from a suggestion, so it reaches the client as suggested and machine fit never learns from its own guess), one update, issued and never awaited (`keepHeldSetup`; the tracker lays it on its own copy of the session first). It used to save them to `clientMachineSettings/_{machineId}` with `clientId: ""`: one ghost record per machine, shared by every open session, read by nobody, never moved to the client. Old ones may still be in production and are harmless (nothing reads an empty client); nothing deletes them.
- **Only an open session holds a set-up**: the hold door is offered only for a session that carries `isUnassigned` (one never a client's), and the rules refuse `heldSetup` on any session that isn't open.
- **The card** (the tracker's `holdSetup` door, `MachineMenuHost.holdSetup`, DialTiles' `hold`) says "Kept on this session · saved to the client when you choose them" (`HELD_SETUP_LINE`) where "Last changed" would be, asks no reason and offers no fit review. Save and Undo keep the whole map for the machine; the toast says "{Machine}: set-up kept on this session · saved to the client when you choose them". No setting history, journal copy or machine-fit row while there is no client.
- **Read back as what is saved** (`heldValuesOf`, `heldAsSettings`, the tracker's `shownSettings`): the card opens on it, the Now Bar's Set up counts it (and is offered in an open session: what the session holds is known), the grid's chips show it, and each set typed after it carries it as its settings.
- **At Assign**, from the bar or at Finish, the held machines are gathered when the batch is built (a set-up kept while the reads were out goes too) and each machine's settings document and history row for the client go INTO THE ASSIGN'S BATCH (`heldMoves`, `queueHeldMoves`, `equipment/mutations.ts` `queueSettingsSave`); `heldSetup` is deleted in the session's write. A held value wins only for the dials it set (`heldOverSaved`), and `dialsOnly` with `writeDials` writes exactly those dials, by name, so the client's other dials stay as the database holds them even when the iPad never read them, and a stale copy can't drop a held one. Each move is signed by whoever kept it (`heldAuthorsOf`, from `byUid`, when the team list knows them; a take-over's first trainer), not by whoever chose the client. Each move's journal copy (at the session's link) and machine-fit row go only once the batch has COMMITTED (`afterCommit`, run in the commit's `then`): a refused assign leaves neither. The client's settings are read beside the sets (the iPad's copy, then the server's for `ASSIGN_SERVER_READ_WAIT_MS` at most) when the session holds a set-up at the tap; only the SERVER's answer, in by the time the batch is built, lets a move claim a first set-up or write the fit row (written whole, it would replace her other dials and reviews with the held ones). The held part is worked out in its own `try`: a move that couldn't go in the batch, or a throw, keeps `heldSetup` on the session and never stops the assign. A refused batch leaves it all on the session, and the card shows it again.
- **An Undo never crosses Assign.** Once the session's assign batch is built, a keep is refused (`heldClosedRef` in the tracker, `heldClosedError`), and an Undo goes where its save went (DialTiles' `SaveTo`), never to the client the card now shows: the card says "Already saved to Judy · change it here", the toast "Leg Press: already saved to Judy · change it on the machine's card" (`heldClosedWords`). A refused batch opens the session to keeps again.
- **After Assign**, until the tracker's settings listener has had the server's answer for the client, the card writes only the dials it changes, no fit row and no first set-up (`MachineMenuHost.settingsUnsure`): the iPad's copy may hold only the dials Assign just moved.
- **No write names an empty client**: `saveSettings` throws before writing with no client, `acknowledgeFlag` too, and `addMachineNote` refuses a note with no client. A note about the client before Who's this? writes nothing and says "Choose who this is first (Who's this?) · your words stay here" with no Try again (`NOTE_NEEDS_CLIENT`); "The machine itself" still files. A card with no client and nowhere to keep its settings reads only.
