# Renewals

Round: **Renewals**, Sep 10–11 2026. The plan is `OPERATIONS-RENEWALS-PROPOSAL.md` in the repo root: §11 records what changed from it, and `RENEWALS-ROUND.md` covers shipping. The business rules are in `docs/business/renewals.md` and `docs/business/packages-and-pricing.md`.

## The idea in one paragraph

A package runs on **two clocks**. Billing sends a payment every 4 weeks (6, 12 or 18 of them) and then auto-renews, where auto-renew is on (`auto-renew.ts` decides: on at the franchise studios and by default, off at the corporate ones). Sessions arrive 8 per payment and never expire. A client who comes 1.5 times a week is still holding sessions when billing finishes. That is the studio's biggest renewal problem, and the reason this feature exists. Every night the app works out both clocks for every client and puts them in exactly **one situation**, always shown as a sentence:

| Situation | Sentence | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| on-track | "9 sessions left · auto-renews Nov 14 · on pace" | — |
| will-bank | "Auto-renews Nov 14 with about 16 sessions still banked" ("Billing ends" when the decided answer is that it won't renew; "Payments finish" when there is no answer — a paid-in-full or banked-sessions lock over a running contract, or a version-1 snapshot. An unmatched package is never will-bank: it is "unknown") | 4+ sessions still banked at the charge (a studio setting). Inside the warning window (30 days) it is "before the charge", unless the decided answer is that it won't auto-renew, or a paid-in-full or banked-sessions lock says nothing bills |
| will-run-out | "Out of sessions around Oct 3, 6 weeks before it renews" ("before billing ends" when it won't renew, "before the payments finish" when there is no answer) | — |
| away | "Snowbird until Apr 1 · clocks paused" | Vacation / Snowbird / Medical, or the MIA pause |
| ended | "Package ended Sep 1 — no new one in Mindbody yet" | Inside the studio's lost window |
| lapsed | "No package since Jul 20" | Past the lost rule: win-back |
| unknown | The first data gap, in words | Not enough Mindbody data to say |

The conversation is due at the studio's threshold (10 sessions left by default). A renewal **already signed in Mindbody** ("on the books") ends it: the client stops being prompted.

**Running low** (Oct 6 2026, AJ: "a way for operations to show how many clients are running out of their sessions ... In total") is the same threshold read on its own: a number at the top of Operations → Renewals, everyone whose `sessionsLeft` is at or under it, talked to or not, with the list behind a tap. It counts from the studio's roster, so a client too slow for any date window is still found; a renewal on the books, a recorded renewal and an Inactive client are left out. `../admin/renewals/running-low.ts`; `docs/rounds/2026-10-06-running-low.md`.

**The lanes read the roster too, by one rule** (Oct 6 2026, the same day, on AJ's yes). Until then the pipeline had its own query, packages ending between six months ago and the planning horizon, so a client with 8 left at a quarter a week, running out next May, never reached Talk now while Operations → Today, counting from the roster, did. Now `../admin/renewals/lanes.ts` places every roster client, and the Pipeline, Today, Week and Month all ask it: the studio's own clients only; an Inactive client (Mindbody's flag, a leader's mark that still holds, or past the studio's Inactive line with nothing booked: Running low's `inactiveOnRecord`) out of Before the charge, Talk now and Coming up; Lapsed and Away untouched (Renewals' lost list stays its own list); Away keeping the old window (AJ: "Keep Away as it is"), except on Month, which lists it by month. `docs/rounds/2026-10-06-talk-now-roster.md`.

## The renewals dashboard's data (Oct 7 2026)

AJ (Oct 6-7 2026) asked for a renewal and retention dashboard: leaders ahead of every renewal, with the exact sessions left (rolled over and given or won included) and what will still be left on the day the commitment ends. The engine's half:

- **Snapshot version 3** (`projection.ts`, through `engine.ts`; the nightly job and `useLiveRenewal` pass the same inputs, the client's `coverage` and the studio's `timeZone` included):
  - `ledger`: sessions left part by part, `carriedIn` (pricing options bought before the current contract began: rolled over) · `thisContract` · `toCome` (8 a payment) · `extra` (the studio's extra-sessions names: complimentary or won, AJ: "Yes added to mindbody"); `total` IS `sessionsLeft`, never a second balance. Said as "52 left: 9 rolled over · 11 this contract · 32 to come · +2 extra" (`ledgerSentence`): extras beside the package's number, as on the profile header. A contract whose whole package Mindbody issued up front on one option bought under it (`issuedUpFront`: its count at least the package's sessions) has nothing to come: its payments' sessions are already on hand.
  - `commitmentEnd`: Mindbody's contract end (`chargeDate`), else the package's start plus its payments × 28 days (paid in full too, estimated).
  - `projection`: `sessionsLeft − booked − pace × paceWeeks` at the end: the 8-week pace, away time left out; a range from the slowest and fastest 4-week pace; the run-out day when it comes first. Below the pace's minimum sample, `leftAtEnd` is null: "Not enough to project yet" (`projectionSentence`), and `paceWeeks` is null too (stored only with a pace, so a snapshot isn't rewritten every night for nothing). `runOutDate` and `bankedAtCharge` are the projection's own, so they never disagree with it. **Bookings**: the dashboard round shipped `MINDBODY_REMAINING_INCLUDES_BOOKED` true, on the guess that Mindbody takes a session off Remaining at booking. **Checked Oct 7 2026 (the Ahead round), it doesn't:** across the four studios, 60 clients whose one pricing option holding sessions showed Remaining plus the visits booked ahead at the pull more than the option ever held (an option of 4 with 4 booked ahead still said 4 Remaining), impossible had a booking taken a session off, and none showed the opposite (`docs/rounds/2026-10-07-ahead.md`). So the constant is **false**: Remaining still counts the booked visits, and they are part of the pace from the day Mindbody counted, never taken off a second time (`booked` stays 0). Taking the known bookings off first, then the pace, is kept and tested (`bookedDaysAhead`, `runOutDay`, `projectAtEnd`) but not switched on: with counts that can be weeks old it would need the visits since the count, which Journey knows only from logged sessions, and it would move the dates night to night as the 30-day booking window slides.
  - `rate`: Mindbody's scheduled charge split by the package, set only when at least two charges agree on it and no other amount is as common (one odd or prorated charge, or a tie, is no evidence); "special" when it differs from the table ("at $54 a session (special)", `rateSentence`); else the package table's, the prepay rate when paid in full. The Brief's Options compare with it (`options.ts` `optionsFor`'s `paying`), so "Vs today's rate" is what the client actually pays.
  - `signals`: the pace's trend (last 4 weeks against the 8 before, never before the first package on file), total sessions with those before Journey (`lib/session-total.ts`), and a package whose rhythm fits the pace better than the current one by at least a quarter of a session a week (`suggestPackage`; nothing when the current package isn't known: no made-up upsell).
- **The day the dates count from, and their ranges** (Ahead, Oct 7 2026; AJ: "2a"). Sessions left is Mindbody's count at its last pull, which can be weeks old, so counting forward from the night of the run put every date later by the count's age. The run-out day, the projection at the end and the conversation's day now count from the day Mindbody counted (`ledger.asOf`; `projection.ts` `projectionStart`, `pipeline.ts` `conversationDueDate`). Mindbody's remaining still counts the booked visits (`MINDBODY_REMAINING_INCLUDES_BOOKED` false, checked Oct 7 2026), so the count is used up at the client's pace from the day it was made: the live version-2 arithmetic, counted from the right day. A client last pulled weeks ago gets an earlier run-out day, and fewer banked at the charge, by about the sessions used since the pull; a client pulled this week barely moves. (Had it been true, the count would be used after the visits held at it, `engine.ts` `heldThroughOf`, measured from the count's day; that arithmetic is kept and tested.) A count made from visits (no pricing options on file) is today's: it starts today. The count the projection uses is the one on the count's day: the on-hand sessions then and 8 for each payment due after that day, so a payment charged between the pull and tonight is never lost from it (sessions left on screen is still tonight's Mindbody number). The conversation's day needs no booking span: its threshold is on Mindbody's own number. A day the pace has already passed says today. The snapshot also keeps `paceRange` (the slowest and fastest 4-week pace) and `runOutRange`, and `conversationDueRange` gives the conversation's, so Ahead can say every date with its range. The 4-week windows end yesterday (the job runs before today's visits), and where their floor is only the 8-week window's own start, which moves a day every night, only whole windows count (`paceRange` `wholeWindows`): otherwise the range came and went by the day of the week. Sessions left on every screen is still Mindbody's number, never counted down.
- **Last talked** is read from the cycle the dashboard already reads (`conversation.ts` `lastTalkOf`, `lastTalkSentence`), not stored on the snapshot, so a talk logged now shows now; a failed read says "Couldn't check".
- **The renewal plan** (`plan.ts`; AJ: "allow in app response", and Strongsville, which doesn't auto-renew, marks who is renewing): `studios/{s}/renewals/{cycleKey}.plan`, set by anyone who works at the studio through `saveRenewalPlan` with one touch of kind "plan" in the same batch. The choices follow the decided auto-renew answer (`planChoicesFor`): renews by itself → Let it renew · Pause billing (only when it banks) · Not renewing · Not decided yet; otherwise → Same package · Upgrading · Downgrading · Pay as you go · Not renewing · Not decided yet. The picker's labels are short, at most 15 characters (`PLAN_LABELS`, `PLAN_LABEL_MAX`), so a closed select on an iPad never cuts one off (AJ, Oct 7 2026: "fix"); the choice said whole (`PLAN_WORDS`: "Let it renew (sessions carry over)", "Pause billing in Mindbody until sessions run low", "Renewing — same package") is the line under the picker while choosing, the plan's sentence once saved, and the conversation history. The stored ids and the rules' list are unchanged. A plan of Let it renew or Pause billing doesn't take a client off Before the charge either (AJ, Oct 7 2026: "keep them there, they will be gone when the month rolls over"). It never touches the outcome; a plan to renew doesn't take a client off Talk now (the new package in Mindbody does, as before); `nextStep` reads it.
- **Names waiting** (`name-suggest.ts`): a suggestion for each Mindbody name nobody has matched, from the studio's own table (sessions and months; PIF and Roll Over noted; comp, free, won → extra sessions; a name with both a package's number and an extra word, "144 Sessions PIF + 2 Free", → none; unsure → none). Confirming goes through the settings form (`settings-form.ts` `assignNameInForm`), which never adds a name past a list's limit (`nameWontFitInForm`: 60 a package, was 20; 40 extras) and the panel names the ones that didn't fit.

## The renewals dashboard's screens (Oct 7 2026)

The screens' half, on the data above:

- **One row, two places** (`RenewalRow.tsx` over `row-facts.ts`): every lane on Operations → Clients → Renewals, Running low's list there (AJ, Oct 7 2026: "Yes"; Left now still says when the sessions run out: Running low's own words on a record with no ledger yet, else the ledger and the run-out day, unless At the end says it, `lowLeftNow`), and My renewals on a trainer's own profile draw the same row, so they never say a client two ways. Per client: their name (opens the Brief, or the profile) and primary trainer; the package with its rate ("Committed · 12 months · at $54 a session (special)"); when the commitment ends with the decided answer ("Auto-renews Jan 29, 2027", "Ends Dec 1", "Ends around Mar 3" for paid in full); sessions left part by part; what will be left when it ends, with its range; who last talked, when and what they heard ("Talked Oct 3 · Jen · Unsure — price"; "Couldn't check" when the conversations didn't read); the one or two signals that matter (`rowSignals`: coming less, nothing booked, a package that fits the pace better, coming more, sessions in all, the next visit booked); and the plan. The working (the projection's arithmetic, when Mindbody's numbers are from, the table's rate beside a special, the proof line) is on the row's (i), never printed on every row.
- **How the row lies out** (`renewal-row.css`, a container query on the list, so it fits Operations with or without its sidebar and My Profile): under 640px one cell under another (a phone, My Profile upright); 640 to 839px two lines of cells with the plan on the right (an iPad upright); 840px and up one line of columns under a header (an iPad on its side), each cell's label kept for screen readers. A cell with nothing to say (no signals, no projection yet) is left out, never filled with "none"; words break between words. Names wrap (`lib/names-wrap.test.ts` holds `.rr__name`, `.rr__trainer` and the plan's sentence); every tap is 40px.
- **The plan picker** (`RenewalPlanPicker.tsx`): a select of the choices that fit the contract (`planChoicesFor`), then the package (when the choice names one) and a note, with Save plan and Cancel; nothing is written by the select alone, and the draft joins the leave warning. It shows who set the plan and when, and the note. Someone who doesn't work at the studio, or a screen whose renewal settings didn't read (the defaults would name the wrong packages), reads the plan without the picker; so does a row whose conversation is still being read ("Checking…") or couldn't be ("Couldn't check"), so a save never replaces a plan nobody saw (`row-facts.ts` `planKnown`). A leader's Decided still asks to record the outcome whatever the plan says. The conversation history says a plan change as "Plan: Upgrading" (`touchHeadline`).
- **Two filters** on the pipeline (`pipeline.ts`): **Plan: not decided** (no plan, or "Not decided yet") and **Not renewing** (the plan says so, or the latest conversation heard it and no plan says otherwise; AJ, Oct 7 2026: "I want to know the people that had the conversation that month so it's not bad to just keep them in the same spot", and one line under the filters says so while it is on, `FILTER_HINTS`). While the conversations are unread both match nobody, never everybody. The tiles, the lanes, Running low and the lane rule (`../admin/renewals/lanes.ts`) are as the Oct 6 rounds built them.
- **The names waiting**: one calm line above the pipeline ("44 names waiting" · Review suggestions, a door to My Studio → Studio), never off settings that failed to read; on My Studio → Studio → Renewals each waiting name says its suggestion ("Suggested: The Trial · paid in full") with **Confirm**, and **Confirm all N** above the list. Confirming puts the name in the form; the save bar writes it, like every change on that screen (leaders only, as before; AJ, Oct 7 2026: "keep" the two taps). At a studio that has never saved a package table of its own, saving a name saves Max Strength's standard table, prices included, as the studio's, so the list says so once first, while a name waits or a package change is unsaved ("{Studio} has no package table of its own yet: saving here makes Max Strength's standard prices {Studio}'s own. ## Decisions

- **Sessions left = sessions on hand (pricing options) + 8 for each payment still to come.** The count is Mindbody's; an estimate says so. Complimentary "Session Comp" sessions count; unmatched pricing options don't, and they show as data gaps. **Given sessions hold the conversation off until they are used too** (AJ, Oct 2 2026: "Yes, count them in"): they are in sessions left, and a client whose package is spent but who still holds given sessions is still using sessions, never "ended" (`sessionsOnly` counts them once a package was recognised). The profile header still splits the two: "36 left in contract · +12 extra" (`sessionsSplit`).
- **Dates are UTC days for Mindbody** (`mindbodyDayKey`) and studio days for everything else.
- **Sessions left is Mindbody's number as of the last pull, never a count-down** (the cost plan, Sep 26 2026). The nightly job counts down (sessions left at the pull, less the sessions Journey logged since) only to decide WHEN to ask Mindbody again - near the studio's conversation threshold, the morning of a day they train, at most weekly. A screen never shows the count-down.
- **Pace** is visits a week over the last 8 weeks, rounded to a quarter. It skips away time and never reaches back before the current package or the studio's first synced booking. It needs 21 observed days.
- **Away beats ended and lapsed.** Snowbirds are not churn.
- **Auto-renew is decided in one place: `auto-renew.ts`** (Sep 25 2026; AJ: "the corporate studios do not have auto renewal on but franchise studio do … its auto default on", and "allow trainers to mark a check box on a profile if the client is on auto renewal"). `decideAutoRenew` takes the first that answers: nothing running or coming, or a coach's lock that says paid in full or banked sessions (`client.contractTierOverride`, `lockSaysNothingBills`) → no answer, whatever Mindbody says (the lock is there because Mindbody's reading is wrong for her); **Mindbody's own flag on the contract** (Mindbody owns contracts); **a trainer's mark on the profile for THIS contract** (`client.autoRenewMark` — a mark from an earlier contract is shown, never used, so it can't silently switch off a later contract's warning); a contract **not matched** to a package in Renewal settings → no answer (a studio's claim is about its own packages, and a confident wrong answer is worse than a missing one); **the package's answer** (`PackageTier.renewsAutomatically`); **the studio's answer** (`RenewalSettings.packagesRenewAutomatically`, My Studio → Studio → Renewals); else **the standard, ON** (`STUDIO_AUTO_RENEW_DEFAULT`). The snapshot (engine version 2) stores the decided `autoRenews`, `autoRenewsFrom` (`"mindbody" | "client" | "package" | "studio" | "default"`; "default" is said as "the standard answer", never "the studio's answer") and `autoRenewsInherited` (the answer without her mark or her lock: what the profile's box shows until someone marks her, and what `renewalOf` rebuilds from once a lock comes off). `billingEndPhrase()` in `sentences.ts` is still the one wording: "auto-renews", "billing ends", or "payments finish" when there is no answer. `chargeWarning` needs a charge, so it is never set when the decided answer is no, nor under a paid-in-full or banked-sessions lock (nothing bills, so no charge is coming — the lock's null answer must not bring back a warning that Mindbody's, the studio's, the package's or a mark's "no" had switched off). So since version 2 a null answer never warns: an unmatched package can't be will-bank (no tier, so "unknown"), and the only other null on a will-bank contract is the lock. The one exception is an old version-1 snapshot, which keeps its warning until the first night rewrites it. `renewalOf` keeps the same rule, so a lock saved today switches the warning off at once. Once the first nightly run has written version 2, the "Before the charge" lane therefore holds only contracts that renew automatically, as its hint says.
- **`renewalOf(client)` is the one single-client accessor** (`auto-renew.ts`): the stored snapshot, re-decided with the client's mark and Mindbody's flag as they are NOW, so a saved tick or a webhook that landed today shows at once on the profile header, the package card, post-session, the briefing, the Hub marker and the progress-report cue. It returns the same object when nothing changed, leaves a version-1 or non-monthly snapshot as it is, and only ever turns a warning OFF (the snapshot doesn't carry the warning window, so turning one on waits for the night). Lists — the pipeline, the Overview, Relay, My renewals, the directory — follow the nightly run. `useLiveRenewal` returns no live snapshot when the settings read failed (a studio switched off must never be worked out as the standard ON) and falls back to `renewalOf(client)`.
- **One snapshot write per change.** The job compares with `sameSnapshot` and writes only what changed. Single-client screens work the snapshot out live (`useLiveRenewal`) instead of calling a server endpoint: the web service has no Firestore admin key.
- **Outcomes** (`outcomes.ts`):
  - A newer package means renewed, upgraded or downgraded (by months). If the newer package has an earlier start, the engine only changed its mind: ignored.
  - The lost rule means lost, but only inside the 180-day lapsed list; anything older is pre-app history.
  - The job takes back its own "lost" if the client returns on the same package.
  - Pay-as-you-go is recorded by a leader.
  - A leader's outcome is never overwritten.
  - `closedOn` is the day the package ended, or the day it closed early.
- **Rates** are for leaders only. They count kept ÷ closed. Pay-as-you-go follows each studio's setting. A trainer needs 5 outcomes before showing, and is credited with the closing package's most-coached visits over its last 90 days.
- **No outreach.** Nothing here contacts a client or a trainer.
