# Story — the page of Notes & Profile

Client codex, Sep 2026 (phase 15 of `docs/rounds/2026-09-24-client-codex.md`). A new page: the long scroll had none. AJ's brief for the profile was *"their history, their journey, who they are, and how to be the best trainer for them"* — and a client's history was scattered over the contract list, the goal history, the Pulse shelf, the InBody card and FORD, each in its own corner. The Story puts the dated moments in one column, **newest first**, and **every line comes from a record somebody already made**: nothing is typed twice, and the page writes nothing.

`story.ts` is the pure half — `buildStory`, the filters, the fold, the since line and the sub-toggle's line — with `story.test.ts` beside it (run it under `TZ=America/New_York`). `StoryPage.tsx` draws it; `client-codex/pages/StoryPage.tsx` is the thin adapter that hands it the tab's story.

## The rules it keeps

- **No reads, and a missing source is named.** The story is built ONCE in the shell (`useCodexData` → `CodexData.story`) from the tab's one load, and the Overview's Story slot will read the same one (phase 18). Each source arrives as a `Src<T>` (`storySourcesOf`, `client-codex/codex-data.ts`): a read still loading is **pending**, one that failed is **failed**, FORD for a reader the rule refuses is **off** — none of them makes a moment, and the page says so ("Still reading notes…", "Couldn't read InBody scans here…", "FORD is kept by the home studio…"). "Nothing recorded yet." only when nothing is unknown. **An empty filter is unknown while a read that feeds it is missing** (`unreadUnder`; `STORY_READ_KIND` files each read under the kind of moment it gives — a new read goes there too): a cross-train reader's Life filter says "Nothing under “Life” that could be read here.", never "yet", and an empty story with only FORD off says "Nothing recorded yet that can be read here." A journal that hit its guard rail says a moment may be missing, and leaves notes and focuses uncertain under their filters.
- **Prior history is real history.** A migrated client is never "new" and never "First session."; the years before Journey are a panel that says they happened. "First Pulse in Journey" only when the Pulse read reached her first report (`AssessmentHistory.complete` — the shell builds it from the RAW progress-reports page at the listener's limit, or `complete` lies). The oldest round of a read that did NOT reach her first report has an unknown baseline and says only "Pulse saved." "No earlier answer changed" only when every statement it answered could be compared, or the read holds her whole Pulse.
- **InBody changes are not stated here.** Whether a difference between two scans is a change is Body & Pulse's call, against the home studio's normal variation (AJ's decision 8). The Story prints what each printout says.
- **Sentences, not scores.** Recorded facts only: no trend, no average, no pace. A Pulse beat quotes words, never 0–10; "2 of 24 statements answered" counts answers (the 24 is `ALL_STATEMENT_IDS.length`, never typed).
- **Verbatim.** A note, a goal, an intent, a FORD line and a Pulse statement are the words as written. A long one folds behind "Read all" (`foldText`: whole sentences when the first of them are long enough to read on their own, else at a space with "…"); it is never reworded. Names are never cut.
- **Neutral.** No crimson, no pillar hue, no hero orange: a milestone's dot is filled in ink, every other dot is an outline. The year is a label beside its moments, not a sticky header (the sub-toggle is the tab's one sticky thing).
- **Dates are days.** A Mindbody contract date is its UTC day; a stored instant is the studio's day; a day key is itself.

## Since when

`storySince` decides the date the page and the sub-toggle both use, and how sure it is:

- **since** — a day the record can stand behind as her start: the prior record's first day (a person stated it), Mindbody's own first visit, or — only when Journey holds her whole story — her first session in Journey; the earliest of them. Else the day Mindbody's record of her was made.
- **since at least** — a day she was certainly with us by, but maybe not the first: the earliest inferred first visit, the earliest contract Journey holds, or the prior record's last day. Given the studio's `today`, a day after it is never a "since" (a `through` typed ahead of the cutover); a 0-session prior record gives no day at all.
- **in Journey since** — only Journey's own dates are known.

The page's line then adds the header's own session numbers (`sessionTotals`, so the two can never disagree): *"With Max Strength since Mar 2019. 412 sessions in FileMaker before Journey, and 49 in Journey."* The where-words are the line's own (`PRIOR_SINCE_WHERE`), each saying "before Journey" once: paper "on paper records before Journey", a trainer's estimate "before Journey (a trainer's estimate)", other just "before Journey". The sub-toggle says the year: "since 2019", "since 2025 or earlier", "in Journey since 2026" (`storyTabHint`).

**Its own function, not the header's `resolveClientSince`**, because the Story has a "since at least" kind and reads the prior record's `from`. `resolveClientSince` takes the EARLIEST proven date (Journey's first session, Mindbody's first visit, Mindbody's created date), and since the landing (Sep 24 2026) it counts Journey's first session only when the caller's coverage is "complete" with no prior record; otherwise it reads "In Journey since". So for a long-standing client whose only date is the day Journey met her, the header and the Story both say "In Journey since".

## The page

The since line, the filters (Everything · Milestones · Body & Pulse · Life · Goals & focus — the prior-record panel is a milestone), what could not be read, then the years. The two newest years are open; an older one is a 40px "8 moments in 2019" button. Each moment is its day (70px column), its words, any Pulse quote ("“I feel stronger than I did 3 months ago.” **Often**, was Sometimes."), and where it came from; a moment with a door is ONE 44px button that opens the card it came from (a note opens its thread on Notes), and "Read all" is its own button beside it. Portrait first: the year sits in an 86px column beside its moments from 640px of page width (every iPad), above them below that.
