## Decisions

- **Schedule is the default and unchanged.** The Hub always opens on Schedule. The grid stays MOUNTED while Opportunities is showing (only `hidden`), so its scroll position and its land-on-now effect are exactly as they were. The strip (the day's two numbers and the seven days) and the Critical-notes banner are shared by both layers, and so is the selected day.
- **Everything is asked about the SELECTED day, never today** — the birthday window, the milestone (her count plus her bookings before it, Operations' `count + i + 1`), the break measured to that booking — so flipping to Thursday shows Thursday's truth. (The Hub card reads the same entries since the calm Hub round; only the briefing still counts from today — see the open items.)
- **One vocabulary, reused, never re-derived**:
  - *Read first* — the Hub's own Critical read (`useHubCriticalNotes`, handed in as `criticalFor`), through `getClientAlertState(client, criticalNotesOn(notes, day))`: the card's exact rule. A client whose notes could not be read claims nothing, and the line under the chips says so.
  - *Watch* — no liability waiver signed (Mindbody's "nw" corner, AJ Sep 28: `waiverState` "not-signed" only, never "not synced yet"), then the last Pulse's red flags, from the same alert state. Clinical history on file is NOT a moment (it would sit on most clients): the entry carries it as `clinicalOnFile`, said quietly in an opened row and the peek.
  - *Welcome* — a consultation (the card's rule); sessions 1–3 when the number may be quoted; first time with this trainer only where Journey holds her whole story (so the trainer tally is every session she has had); back after a break measured in **missed sessions at her own pace** (`renewal.pacePerWeek`; about 3 or more), claimed only inside the part of her timeline Journey owns (`ownedWindow` / `canClaimGap`).
  - *Celebrate* — a milestone from Operations' ONE list (`SESSION_MILESTONES`: 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400, 500, 750, 1000…) only when `canQuoteSessionNumber`; a birthday within a week either side, "turns N" only when the birth year is on file.
  - *Renew* — `renewalPromptDue`, in the Wrap-up's own words (`promptText`).
  - *Get to know* (wave 2 hub, Sep 28 2026) — the ✎ "Ask about" from FORD (`get-to-know.ts`), from the Hub's one FORD read (`fordFor`, handed in as `criticalFor` is): a detail whose day comes round within a week of the booking's day, or noted in the two weeks before it; one per client. The chip ("Ask: the recital · Sat") is the list's, the sentence ("Ask about: … (Family, noted Sep 10).") the peek's and the opened row's under "Something to say"; on the grid the mark is the glyph alone, labelled only "Something to ask about". Last in the Key's order, so a busy card folds it into "+N" first. A client whose FORD couldn't be checked is `askUnknown`: the line under the chips says so once, and her opened row and peek say so rather than "Nothing special today." AJ's two open questions keep the design's defaults: a dated detail that has just happened doesn't count, and a "Follow up next time" question doesn't count.
- **Noise rules** (§7): one sentence per row in ONE fixed column; at most three chips (`MAX_ROW_CHIPS`), Read first always first, and never the fact the sort already states; filter chips carry counts and a zero is not drawn; "Can't tell yet" is gathered at the bottom, folded, with its count — never a "0", a "#1" or "New" for a client we can't number.
- Colour by family, shape by family (the Key): Read first crimson (the Critical note's own colour — the only red, as on the card), Watch plum, Welcome blue, Celebrate orange, Renew green, Get to know blue with its own shape (the speech bubble, `MessageCircle`).
- Studio / Mine: Mine is booked with you that day, or coached by you in the last 60 days (`renewal.coachIds`). Sort, filter and scope are remembered on the iPad (local storage; a sign-out clears it).

## Reads

None of its own beyond the studio's package table (`useRenewalSettings`, the one document the profile and the directory read), so Left says the profile's number. Bookings, roster, sessions and the Critical notes are the Hub's. **No per-client query.**

**The studio's FORD, once per studio visit** (wave 2 hub, Sep 28 2026; AJ: "all yes"): `use-hub-ford.ts` over `ford/hub-read.ts`. ONE collection group query, the Delight queue's kind: `studioId == S` and (an annual day, whatever year · a one-off day inside the strip's two weeks · noted in the last two weeks) — the three reasons a detail can come up on any strip day (`askReadWindow`), never the studio's whole FORD. One `getDocs`, no listener; held until the studio or the studio day changes or someone signs out, so a detail noted today is asked about from the next visit. Opened only for someone who works at the studio (`mayReadWeeks`); a client visiting from another studio has her FORD at her home studio and is not in it. Indexes: `ford` collection group (studioId, recurrence) and (studioId, occurredAt), new; (studioId, eventDate), existing. No rules change (the ford block already lets the studio's people read it).

## Built, and waiting for a read (hub cherry round, Sep 28 2026)

Two rules were built and tested here and deliberately **not wired**, because the data each needs is in nothing the Hub holds, and the reads that would bring it need AJ's OK (the house rules: no new query shape or index, no new stored field, no scan). **AJ said yes to both** ("all yes", Sep 28 2026): Get to know is wired in wave 2 hub (see Decisions and Reads above; `docs/rounds/2026-09-28-hub-2.md`). What the cherry round wrote:

- **All stars** (`all-stars.ts`). AJ: "a category for our all stars for the clients that come 2 times a week almost every week of the year for over 6 months". The rule proposed back to him: twice a week on average, in at least 9 of every 10 weeks, for the last 26 weeks — read as the 26 whole weeks before today (today never decides it), a visit in at least 24 of them, about twice a week over the weeks she came (to the nearest quarter, as the renewals pace is). Given only where Journey holds every visit in the window (`ownedWindow` reaches back past its first day, and so does the record the visits came from); otherwise "can't tell", and no screen ever says who isn't one. **What it needs:** 26 weeks of each booked client's visits. The app streams one day of sessions, the Hub's bookings run from yesterday to a week ahead, and the nightly renewals job reads 90 days (its proof counts 12 weeks). The two ways to wire it: the nightly job keeps the answer on the client (a longer read for that job and a new stored field — a Firestore structure change), or the Hub reads 26 weeks of a studio's sessions (a scan the house rules refuse). **Where it would show:** an "All stars" section beside New · Building · Regulars on the Sessions sort (`ALL_STARS_LABEL`), and `allStarWords` ("All star: in 25 of the last 26 weeks, about twice a week.") in the peek and the opened row.
- **Get to know** (`get-to-know.ts`), the ✎ "Ask about" mark (research-hub §5 and §7): a FORD detail whose day comes round within the week (the day and the six after it; an annual one rolled forward from its digits on the studio's day) or noted in the last two weeks; never archived, the In one line document, a legacy row, a detail no longer true, or a closed window; one per client, the soonest dated one first; the chip is the detail's subject or the trainer's first words ("Ask: the recital · Sat"), the sentence every word with its proof. **What it needs:** the studio's FORD details, read once for the Hub. The one studio-wide FORD read Journey makes — the Delight queue's (`ford/useClientFord.ts`, `studioId` + `opportunity.status in [idea, planned]`) — gives only details with an open gesture, so it cannot feed this. Every other shape is new: all of a studio's details (a big read on every Hub open), the dated ones by `eventDate` (an index exists, but a range misses an anniversary stored in an earlier year), the new ones by `occurredAt` (no index). `clients/{id}.fordSummary.nextDate` would cost no read, but it is FORD text on the client document, which cross-train studios can read, and whether it keeps being written is waiting on AJ (ford/README). **How it would wire:** a sixth family, "get-to-know" (Get to know, blue, `MessageCircle`), after Renew in `FAMILY_ORDER` and `FILTERS`; `askAboutByClient` over the read's details and the day's booked ids, handed to the engine the way `criticalFor` is; the card shows the glyph alone (FORD details never on the grid), the peek and the opened row say the sentence, and the Key gains its row.

## Deliberately out of scope

- **Surgery / away within 14 days** from dated notes and FORD: the FORD half could ride on Get to know's read, the dated notes could not (a journal read of its own), and neither was asked for in wave 2. The card's Away and Medical chips still read the legacy `client.events`.
- **"Show on schedule"** (the list has no scroll target on the grid yet). The Key sheet, the card peek, the chips that spotlight the grid and the grid clean-up itself are built: `features/hub-schedule/`.
- **A private "seen"** per opportunity (§7 rule 12).
