SOME CONTEXT TO CONSIDER:

Amounts listed are suggested starting weights for a new client OR a new
exercise for an existing client. It should be relatively challenging but within
their control.

"Novice" would indicate that the client is new to resistance training and has
what would be estimated as below average strength. They may also be considered
intensity adverse and/or possibly have a relatively sedentary lifestyle.

"Advanced" would indicate that this client has some resistance training
experience and/or is naturally strong and active, although still relatively new
to MSF.

Importantly, if a client is new to resistance training but is also relatively
fit and active, we may determine to start them between the Novice and Advanced
ranges. Additionally, if someone is severly deconditioned or has an injury
history, we may determine that we should start lower than the Novice level and
progress slowly.

THESE SUGGESTIONS ARE ALSO BASED ON FRESH STRENGTH LEVELS. ANY EXERCISES
PERFORMED PRIOR TO THESE IN THE SAME WORKOUT MAY CREATE A A NEED FOR A
REDUCTION IN WEIGHT OF THE LATER EXERCISES.
