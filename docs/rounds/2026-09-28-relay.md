## What was built

**Phase 1 — one header** (`2b2120a`). My Studio's masthead, Relay's tabs and the Now Bar were three bars, about a quarter of an upright iPad before any work. They are one (`my-studio/StudioHeader`): "My Studio · RELAY ▾" (its menu holds the five sections, a tap, never a hover), Relay's tabs, the time (the shift, minutes free, the next session; a tap unfolds the day strip), **Tracking** (the one job you took, `relay/board/tracked.ts`, module memory, nothing stored), **Ask** and **+**. One row on a landscape iPad, two upright. The floating Capture button went: Ask asks the team, + holds your own to-do, reminder or note (and a leader's studio task or team job). "Just now" is a still list on the Board with a heart on each line (`JustNow.tsx`), never hidden.

**Phase 2 — the Board** (`ddac5d7`). The Floor tab answers one question first: what should I do with the time I have? **Right now** is one sentence with its proof and the door it opens (`right-now.ts`: the studio closed → Mine; a cover nobody has → Help a teammate; handed to you → Mine; a new initiative today → From leadership; today's list can't be counted → no door, and it says so; a quiet floor → Floor work; otherwise Desk work). **Five doors** (Floor work · Desk work · Help a teammate · From leadership · Mine) say how many and how long (`doors.ts`). **Dealt to you**: the one job behind the open door that fits best, the biggest words on the screen, Take it and Not now, two more that also fit and Deal me another. Just now and **Team today** (the studio's chores by part of the day) beside it, **Later today** (your next gaps, when Closing opens) under it, and behind each door the Floor's own lanes, their logic unchanged.

**Phase 3 — cards and names** (`2b10945`). Every target 44px, no red anywhere on the Board. **Who?** (leaders only, q5): faces in the order of who can help now (free until 2:40, with a client until 3:00, on from 4:00…), in name order within each, and a name on a chore for today, this week or two weeks (`who.ts`). **I can't** on work with your name on it: an ask goes back on the board, a team job lets you step off, a chore only a leader may un-name opens an ask to the team. **Every pass-over, Done and swipe has an Undo** for eight seconds. Capture's Someone is a leader's; a trainer's "someone" becomes an offer; Mine's "Offer it".

**Phase 4 — the tab rename** (`bb58c9b`). Board · Tracker · Journal, labels and words only.

**Phase 5 — the Tracker** (`c92723b`). Mine sorted work by where it came from, in eight lanes; the Tracker sorts it by when (`tracker.ts`): **Today** (Handed to you · Now · Follow-ups · Closing), **Coming up**, **Anytime**, **Someday · Growth**, **Done**, over the data that already existed. A rail beside the list (above it upright): what you're tracking with a door to the Board, the lists with their counts, + To-do, New reminder and All my tasks. A leader's assignment is simply yours: Done and I can't, each with its Undo. An empty list is said only once every read has answered. `myTaskBuckets` and `handedAsks` went with Mine.

**Phase 6 — Opening and Close out** (`6bcbeae`). Two small cards under Right now (`shift-cards.ts`, `ShiftCards.tsx`). **Opening**, from 90 minutes before your first session until it starts (or through the studio's opening shift): the opening chores, what's handed to you, cover asks and your first session, each line with its door; only lines with something behind them, and no card with none. **Close out**, once your last session has ended (or in the closing shift for someone with nothing booked): what's still open on today's list, from the Tracker's own lists, each with the write it always had and an Undo (back to the board, hand back, step off, move a one-off to tomorrow, ask the team), and **your day so far** in facts. "Got it" folds a card for the day on this iPad; a line at the foot of Later today says when each card is, brings it back, and previews Close out early. The shift rings stopped counting a trainer's own to-dos as the studio's chores (one private to-do could make "Mid chores 1 of 4" out of 3).

**Phase 7 — the Ask sheet, and the open-questions trail** (`98c2381`). **Ask the team** (`AskSheet.tsx`, `ask.ts`): six typed tiles, each asking only for what it needs. *Cover me* (the client, the day, anything to know; the session's time is said in the ask's words, "Cover Hamfast Gamgee at 4:00 PM", and kept nowhere else), *A hand on the floor* (now or at a time, where; gone at the end of the day), *Hand this off* (a leader names the person and rings their bell once; a trainer's is an offer), *A question* (for the team, or a leader's one person), *Something's broken* (the Floor Map's flag and the leader's bell it always rang; whether it can still be used is said in the flag's words), *Other*. It opens from where the need is: the header's Ask, **"I need cover"** on a session still to come on the day strip, **"Ask"** on a client's task on Desk work, and a row of the six tiles behind Help a teammate. Typing is on the leave warning's list; a failed post says so and keeps everything.

**The open-questions trail.** A question about one client opens a thread on her record, written by the asker's own iPad (the new journal kind `"question"`, Heads up while it's open, "Open question from Ioreth" on every card, the briefing's included); the ask keeps the thread's id (`taskRequests.threadId`, the one approved field); every reply, every take-over ("Beregond took it on.") and the answer ("Answered: …") is written onto the thread **by the person doing it**, and the answer closes it. `studio-tasks/question-trail.ts` is the one place the two meet, through `client-notes/thread-write.ts`: the board write goes first; a record line that fails says so and loses nothing; a thread written before the board failed is kept for the retry, so her record never gets the question twice. A question is **answered, not ticked**: the Board deals it with "Answer", the Tracker opens its answer box beside the list, the asks lane names the client ("About Nancy Took · on her record until it's answered") and keeps a closed question's answer on screen ("Answered lately"). On her Notes page the whole exchange reads oldest first and moves to Resolved when answered. It is filed under Coaching tip, never Preference and never unfiled, so it never lands in the Wrap-up's To-file tray. Notifications from a card now name the Auth uid, which the rules pin.

## What this round wrote, and what it didn't

- **One new stored field**, approved: `taskRequests.threadId`. **One new stored value**, approved: `journalEntries.kind = "question"`.
- Nothing else new is stored. Tracking, Not now, the list you're on, "Got it" on Opening and Close out and "opened on this initiative" are module memory on the iPad, and a sign-out forgets each (`forgetOnSignOut`).
- **No change** to `firestore.rules`, `firestore.indexes.json`, `functions/`, `server/`, the Mindbody integration or any other part of the Firestore structure. No new collection, no new query, no per-client read in a loop. The question's thread is written with the rules as they are: `journalEntries` pins only `authorId` to the signed-in uid and keeps the client; `taskRequests` restricts no keys.
- **Nothing pings anyone new.** The only bells are the ones that already rang: a hand-off for the person named, a claim and a close for the asker, a flagged machine for the studio's leader.
