# Ahead — Operations, everything past this week · Oct 7 2026

Branch `oct7/ahead`, on master's `52108b56` (the renewals dashboard, live). The folder's own account is `src/features/admin/ahead/README.md`.

## The ask

AJ pasted a brief on Oct 7 2026: Operations shows when a week is all clear, but the data is cramped and hard to move through. Once a leader sees a week is handled, there is no easy way to look further ahead or into where upcoming clients stand. He asked for a calendar and timeline view of renewals and retention, like the client history's, so leaders can see where every active and upcoming client stands. The brief asked for four things: the design, what leaders get out of it, the component plan, and how to read the data cheaply.

The answer was a proposal with a working iPad prototype (the private artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/6T37HKpS6P676fRN744hXR). It named four causes:
- time is cut into pages that stop;
- the dates exist on last night's record, but no screen lines them up;
- Running low and MIA are counts as of today, not dates;
- the far dates drift, because they count forward from tonight on a Mindbody count that can be weeks old.

It ended with three questions.

## The review's fixes

A fresh review of the branch found eight problems, each checked against the code and fixed:

1. **A count made from visits got the booking span.** A client with no pricing options on file has sessions left worked out from visits, which never took a booking off; the new rule still pushed their dates past their bookings (up to 30 days, and a wrong Before the charge was possible). Now such a count starts today, as version 2 did.
2. **A payment between the pull and tonight was lost from the projection.** The pull's on-hand sessions don't have it and tonight's payments still to come don't count it. The projection now counts the payments due after the count's day (`countAtCount`); sessions left on screen is unchanged.
3. **A trainer remembered from another studio could empty Ahead**, with the picker showing Everyone and no way to clear it. A trainer who isn't anyone's primary trainer here now reads as Everyone.
4. **A regular's run-out day hopped every night between pulls**, because the booking span was measured from tonight. It is now measured from the count's day (`heldThroughOf`), and a test holds it still from night to night.
5. **The pace range came and went by the day of the week.** The job runs before the day's visits, and the oldest window was cut short by the 8-week window's moving start. The windows now end yesterday, and only whole windows count where the floor is that moving start (`paceRange` `wholeWindows`). This slightly narrows the range Renewals already shows for some clients.
6. **A coach's paid-in-full mark on a monthly contract** said "Payments finish, nothing has answered". It now says nothing bills, and why.
7. **Week ahead's Further ahead could name a week Ahead shows empty** (it didn't read the conversations), and said "nothing to decide" before the roster had loaded. It now uses Ahead's own reads (`useAheadFrom`, on the Journey Week already holds) and waits for them.
8. **May slip skipped a client too new to judge**, who still crosses At risk with nothing booked. Now it's said for them too; any other Unknown still says nothing.

## What 2a changes in numbers leaders already see

The Renewals rows read the same nightly record, so they move with it the first night after this ships:

- **A regular's run-out day moves later, and their sessions banked at the charge go up, by about how far ahead they were booked when Mindbody counted.** Mindbody already took those booked visits off its count, and the old arithmetic used the count up from today as if it still held them. A twice-a-week client with four weeks of standing bookings banks about 8 more at the charge than the row said before. Before the charge can pick up a client it missed, and Running low's order can shift.
- **A client last pulled weeks ago gets an earlier run-out day than before** (the count is used from the day it was taken, not from tonight). Near the end of a package the job pulls weekly, so it is a few days at most there.
- The conversation's day counts from the pull's day and needs no booking span: the studio's number is on Mindbody's own count, which falls with the bookings.

**This rests on one fact that is still unchecked**, the first of the renewals dashboard's checks: does a pricing option's Remaining go down when a visit is booked? The ship script asks for it before it pushes. If it goes down (as built), ship as is. If it doesn't, stop and tell Claude: the one switch flips to `false`, and the arithmetic for that answer is already built and tested.

## Left out on purpose

- **The look back.** The proposal scrolled up a year for what was decided, to replace Month's. With Month staying (1b), Month and Week → Last week keep the look back, and Ahead starts at this week.
- **"All my studios."** Ahead is one studio at a time, like Month. An owner's view of several studios would read one summary document per studio from the nightly job (`watch/ahead`), not three rosters; it is written down, not built.
- **Trainers' own clocks** on My Profile → My renewals: not asked, so not built.
- **The run-out range on the Renewals rows.** The rows get the corrected dates; the range shows on Ahead and in its panel.
- **Bookings beyond 30 days.** Journey keeps 30 days of bookings; May slip stops there and says so ("bookings to here" on the Clients axis).

## No change to

Firestore rules, indexes (no new query shape), Cloud Functions, Mindbody calls or cadence, the session record. The only structure change is AJ's 2a: two optional job-written fields on `clients/{id}.renewal` (`paceRange`, `runOutRange`). Nothing contacts anyone.

## Deploy

`scripts/ship/ship-ahead.ps1`, from `.claude\worktrees\ahead`: `-Stage prepare`, then `-Stage golive`. It expects master on GitHub at exactly `52108b56` and stops otherwise. Before golive, the Mindbody check above.

golive tags `restore/2026-10-07-before-ahead` and pushes, fast-forward only. Then, by hand on Render (a push deploys nothing there):
- **Manual Deploy** of the web service;
- **Manual Build** of `journey-cron-renewals`, which carries the engine change, and of `journey-cron-leaderboards`, to keep all three on one commit.

The renewals job's next run (2:30 AM Eastern) rewrites each snapshot with the new fields. Until it does, Ahead draws the dates the record already has, one day each, without ranges.

To undo: `git push --force origin restore/2026-10-07-before-ahead:master`, then the same three Render buttons. The older job ignores the new fields; nothing else is stored.
