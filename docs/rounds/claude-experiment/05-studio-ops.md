## 1. What this area does — my interpretation

**Where a studio is run: My Studio.** The bottom-bar tab `studio-tasks` mounts `src/features/my-studio/MyStudioView.tsx`, which owns a masthead with four sections — Relay · Machines · Team · Studio — plus two "doors" every section can open: the Capture sheet (`relay/board/CaptureSheet.tsx`) and the Context Panel (`relay/board/ContextPanel.tsx`), both handed down through `RelayProvider` (`MyStudioView.tsx:178`). Which section an iPad was on is module memory (`section-memory.ts`), and Team/Studio are hidden unless `reachesTier(authTrainer, studioId, "leads")` → `leadsHere()` → `canManageRenewals()` in `src/features/renewals/permissions.ts` (yes: the app-wide "runs this studio" test lives in the Renewals feature). **Relay** (`relay/PlannerView.tsx`) is four tabs — Floor · Mine · Notes · Network — under a Now Bar that ticks once a minute off the app's 3-day schedule window. The Floor tab is `studio-tasks/StudioHubView.tsx`: Next-up cards, shift rings, the shift strip (templates × instances × machines, derived by `recurrence.ts` `planDay`), the Floor Map (machine wear from today's `sessionMachineIds` + `studios/{s}/machineCare`), the team-jobs lane, client tasks, a renewals lane, the requests board (`taskRequests` + replies + reactions + initiatives) and the playbook. Mine is the trainer's personal templates/instances (`trainers/{uid}/task*`), hand-offs, follow-ups and growth items. Notes is a private document system (`trainers/{uid}/notes` + folders, working log, sources, markdown-lite) with three share modes: onto a client's record (`clients/{id}/sharedNotes`), to colleagues (`studios/{s}/noteShares`, people or team), and to every MSF studio (a team share fanned out per studio). **Machines** (`my-studio/MachinesSection.tsx`) is the roster editor (`admin/machines/StudioInventoryManager.tsx` on `studios/{s}/roster`), "New in the MSF standard", "Shared by other studios", and a machine "door" that stacks the legacy Studio setup card, the floor's notes, Local set-up, Upkeep and "Offer to the MSF catalog" (`catalogSubmissions`). **Team** is Relay's old Team cockpit (`relay/team/TeamPanel.tsx`: accountability records, open loops, cohorts, the vault, the task manager) plus the staff panel (`admin/staff/useStaffRoster` + `StaffEditor`, approvals into this studio, the grant). **Studio** is the studio's own record (`admin/studios/StudioDetailsForm`), a sync sentence, the studio's day (shift hours, deep-clean interval, session minutes), the renewal settings panel and a studio-only announcement composer.

**Where a studio is looked at: Operations.** `src/features/admin/AdminDashboardView.tsx` is a nine-tab shell wrapped in `OperationsScopeProvider` (`scope-context.tsx`): "Looking at: this studio · All my studios", where "this studio" is literally the app's active studio (`pickStudio` calls `setActiveStudioId`, `scope-context.tsx:84`) and "all" is `operationsStudios()` (`scope.ts:40`). It is reachable by `isStudioLeader(authTrainer)` (`src/AppContent.tsx:1403`, role-only). Tabs: **Overview** (`overview/OverviewPage.tsx`: today's five tiles, a Needs-you strip, eight foldable panels — changes today, next three days, pain and critical notes, renewals, attendance watch, moments, strength dropped, team this week — and four one-line "doors"; its own reads are a live week of `schedules` including cancellations (`changes/useWeekSchedule.ts`, two listeners), four one-shot reads (`overview/useOverviewReads.ts`), the watchlist and acknowledgements streams (`attention/useAttention.ts`), the Delight collection-group query, renewal settings and per-30-client cycle listeners (`renewals/usePipeline.ts:69`), a 14-day paged sessions read (`sessions-range.ts`) and the machine-fit index with an in-browser Kaizen build (`machine-fit/useFitFloor.ts:63`)); **Renewals** (pipeline lanes from the nightly `client.renewal` snapshot, the Brief, outcomes — settings live on My Studio); **Delight queue** (`ford/DelightQueue`); **Floor** (the SAME `MachinesSection`, Machine fit, Routines); **Staff & Roles**; **Insights** (sentences over a 7/30/90-day sessions window, Hours inside); **Announcements**; **Mindbody** (a leader's own studio; administrators the estate); **Data** (two CSV exports). Under "All my studios" six of the nine tabs render `PickOneStudio`; only Overview (→ `network/NetworkOverview.tsx`, a sync-health list), Staff & Roles and Hours span.

**Where the app is managed: the Admins dashboard.** `src/features/admins/AdminsDashboardView.tsx` (Admin/Founder only via the app's `isAdmin`, computed in `src/App.tsx:71-76` as Admin|Founder — not Overseer) re-mounts the corporate screens: All locations (`admin/studios/AdminStudiosTab.tsx`, which also mounts a THIRD floor-editing surface, `equipment/StudioEquipmentPanel.tsx`), Catalog (`admin/machines`), Standard template, Limbo, System tools (`admin/system/AdminSystemToolsTab.tsx` — reorder trainers, rebuild rollups, restore machines, and **the wipe**, which is still `executeAppCleanse` in `src/AppContent.tsx:943` deleting eleven collections from the browser behind a typed phrase), Bug reports and Data (any studio).

**Renewals** is the cleanest slice: one pure engine (`renewals/engine.ts` `buildRenewalSnapshot`) run by the nightly Render job (`server/renewals-job.ts`) to write `clients/{id}.renewal`, and re-run live for one client in the browser (`useLiveRenewal.ts`) with three bounded reads. Its package rules match `docs/business/packages-and-pricing.md` exactly (`settings.ts:21-56`: 6/12/18 payments, 48/96/144 sessions, $70/60/54, $560/480/432, prepay 67/57/51; `engine.ts:675` = sessions on hand + payments-left × 8). The screens compute only lane placement (`pipeline.ts laneOf`), never the snapshot. **Calendar** (`features/calendar`) is read-only month/week/day over the schedule window. **Trainer profile** shows rollups written by Cloud Functions, the Kaizen roster (an array on the trainer doc), My renewals, and an `EditTrainerModal` on shadcn. **Comments** (`features/comments`) are studio-scoped threads with @tags under Learning pages. **Notifications** are per-trainer bell documents written from the sender's device (`notifications/mutations.ts`), 14 kinds; reminders are personal tasks with a time that the trainer's own iPad rings (`relay/reminders/useReminderBell.ts`) and toasts on whatever screen is open (`PlannerReminders.tsx`).

**How it moves, for the two people it serves.** A trainer taps My Studio, lands on Relay's Floor (Next up, rings, the strip), swipes a card or taps Capture (one composer that writes to one of four models: a personal template, a studio template, a request/hand-off, or a team job — `relay/board/capture.ts:317-393`). A studio leader taps the same tab for Team (let a hire in, post a job, read who is behind) and Studio (edit the record, hours, renewal packages), then flips App Mode to Operations to read the Overview, acknowledge pain rows, snooze attendance rows, open the renewals pipeline, or pull the schedule. Roughly **62 K non-test lines** (my count: 61,960 across the ten folders) sit behind those two journeys — about half of all feature code — and they are reached by nobody during a set.

2. **Tier/role gating.** The "can this person" question is answered by **ten** different definitions of the role sets: `lib/permissions.ts` (`isStudioLeader` :76 inheritance chain; `isFranchiseOwner` :108 returns `isFounder` and is dead), `renewals/permissions.ts:17-19` (SUPER/FRANCHISE/LEADER + `leadsStudio`), `learning/permissions.ts:28-30` (a second copy: `leadsStudioPerRules`), `admin/scope.ts:37` (a third SUPER set), `admin/franchise/scope.ts` (`isSuperAdminRole`), `admin/staff/StaffEditor.tsx:57-61` (tier lists), `trainer-profile/visibility.ts:100-101`, `relay/notes/access.ts:60`, `admin/AdminDashboardView.tsx:144` (inline `isOwnerTier`), and `src/App.tsx:71-76` (`isAdmin` = Admin|Founder). They do not all agree: the rules' `roleIsSuper` (`firestore.rules:209-211`) includes **Overseer**; the app's `isAdmin` does not, so an Overseer can write the catalog by rule but cannot open the Admins dashboard (`AdminsDashboardView.tsx:73`). Two screen gates the rules refuse: (a) Operations → Staff & Roles offers a head trainer `OWNER_TIER_ROLES` on approval (`AdminStaffTab.tsx:194`) while `mayHandOut` (`firestore.rules:269-273`) lets a studio-tier caller mint only studio-tier roles; (b) Operations → Mindbody shows every leader "Send a test event" (`AdminMindbodyTab.tsx:563-570`) while `/api/mindbody/test-webhook` is in `ADMIN_ONLY_MINDBODY_PATHS` (`server.ts:186`). 3. **Relay/studio-tasks data model.** Sixteen collections: `studios/{s}/taskTemplates`, `taskInstances`, `taskCategories`, `taskRequests` (+`replies`, +`submissions/{trainerId}`), `teamJobs`, `noteShares`, `machineCare`, `vault`, `playbook`, `upkeepLog`; `trainers/{uid}/taskTemplates`, `taskInstances`, `notes`, `noteFolders`, `notifications`; `clients/{id}/sharedNotes`; `networks.relayFocus`. 4. **The Overview's sources.** Live: the week's schedule (2 listeners), watchlist (1), acknowledgements (1, newest 600), Delight (1 collection-group, limit 200), renewal settings (1), cycles (`ceil(N/30)` `documentId() in` listeners, `usePipeline.ts:80-97`), the machine catalog (1). ## 2. Anomalies — the irregular, clunky and suboptimal

1. **Critical — `src/features/admin/equipment/LocalSetupDialog.tsx:83-87` + `clone.ts:141,143`.** The Local set-up dialog (reachable from the machine door on My Studio → Machines, Operations → Floor, and Admins → All locations) writes `buildClone(...)` with `setDoc(..., { merge: true })`. `buildClone` sets `status: "active"` unconditionally (`clone.ts:141`) and omits `overrides`/`studioNotes`/`unit` when they are blank (`:143-147`). Consequences, both confirmed by running `buildClone` with blank inputs: saving a note on a machine that is **out of service flips it back to active**; clearing "What this studio calls it" or the unit notes **silently keeps the old value** (deep merge). This is precisely the trap `docs/KNOWN-TRAPS.md` recorded the same day ("Write a roster override with `updateDoc`, never `setDoc(..., {merge:true})`") and that `StudioMachineEditor.tsx:145-160` already follows. *Why it matters:* a leader marks the leg press broken, adds "pin sticks", and the floor sees it as active again. *Direction:* one writer for roster overrides (the editor's `updateDoc` path), preserve `entry.status`, and delete `buildClone`'s status line.

2. **High — `src/features/equipment/adapters.ts:385-386` vs `catalog/StudioSetupCard.tsx:116-117` vs `admin/machines/StudioMachineEditor.tsx:145-160`.** One machine at one studio now has **two "standard settings" stores** edited from the **same door**: the Studio setup card writes legacy `studioMachineSettings/{studio}_{machine}.standardSettings` (ARCHITECTURE §3.2 marks the collection legacy, "0 documents on Sep 12"), the machine editor writes `studios/{s}/roster/{m}.overrides.defaultSettings`. The floor's reader gives the legacy document precedence (`studioMachineSettings?.[id] || machine.standardSettings || catalog?.defaultSettings`), so a value set in the new editor is beaten by an older card value nobody can see is winning. *Direction:* finish the §3.5 migration — the card becomes a view of the roster override, and the legacy collection loses its writer.

3. **High — `src/features/admin/overview/useOverviewReads.ts:48-54` + `questions.ts:240-254` + `OverviewPage.tsx:455`.** The Overview's "N open incidents" reads `clinicalIncidents`, a legacy collection with **no writer left in `src/`** (grep: only readers). Incidents written today go to `journalEntries` with `kind: "incident"` (the composer) or to the leaders-only vault (`relay/board/vault.ts`), neither of which this panel counts as an incident; a journal incident surfaces only if it is also `importance: critical`, and then as a "critical note". Monday question #4 ("instant visibility of any reported injury", ARCHITECTURE §1.5) is answered from a dead table. The read is also `limit(100)` with no order, so a studio with more than 100 legacy rows gets an arbitrary hundred. *Direction:* read journal incidents (one query, `studioId + kind + resolvedAt == null`), drop the legacy read, and decide whether the vault's staff incidents belong on the leader's Overview at all.

4. **High — `src/features/admin/data/useStudioExports.ts:119-131`.** "Client attendance" CSV queries `schedules` for the date range **with no studio clause** and filters by `studioId` in memory — the whole company's bookings for a month are pulled to the iPad to export one studio (the sibling sessions query at `:88-95` was fixed for exactly this; this one was not). It also builds `new Date("yyyy-mm-dd")` then `setHours(0,0,0,0)` (`:114-117`), the UTC/local date trap. And "Sessions by trainer" stamps `Session Date` from `createdAt` (`:166`), so a session logged late gets the wrong day on a pay sheet — the exact trap Hours avoids ("the tally files by the session's date"). *Direction:* scope the query, take the date from `s.date`, and share `fetchSessionsInRange` with Hours.

5. **High — `src/AppContent.tsx:943-985` + `admin/system/AdminSystemToolsTab.tsx:176-183`.** The database wipe is still a browser action on production data behind one typed phrase (already known, ARCHITECTURE §2.8 #7 and §5.3 Gate B — still true on Sep 20). New detail: it deletes `trainers` and `studios` (every account) but not `journalEntries`/`progressReports`/`ford`, so the "wipe" would strand health text readable by any signed-in user; its dialog promises to re-create the machines and the code does not; and `Promise.all(deleteDoc × N)` on `exerciseLogs` will hit write limits half-way and leave a torn database. *Direction:* an admin-only server route behind an environment check, or delete the button before the first trainer signs in.

6. **High — `src/features/admin/staff/AdminStaffTab.tsx:194-195` vs `firestore.rules:269-273`.** On Operations → Staff & Roles a head trainer (who can open Operations) is offered `OWNER_TIER_ROLES` — Studio Owner and Owner — when approving someone, because the list is chosen by `isAdmin` alone; `mayHandOut` refuses anything above the studio tier for a studio-tier caller, so the approval fails after the form. The same person on My Studio → Team gets `STUDIO_TIER_ROLES` and `canChangeRole` (`TeamSection.tsx:171-174`); on Operations `canChangeRole={isAdmin}` locks role changes entirely. Two doors, one editor, two answers — the thing `StaffEditor.tsx:1-7` says cannot happen. *Direction:* derive `assignableRoles`/`canChangeRole` from `leadsHere`/tier once, in `StaffEditor`, not at each door.

7. **High — `src/features/studio-tasks/StudioHubView.tsx:191-194` → `requests.ts:365` vs `firestore.rules:2250`; also `admin/announcements/AdminAnnouncementsTab.tsx:107` → `audience.ts:226` vs `firestore.rules:747`.** The board's ask box, JobSheet `me`, playbook confirms and Operations → Announcements sign the author with `authTrainer.id`; the rules pin `createdBy.id`/`authorId` to `request.auth.uid`. Capture (`CaptureSheet.tsx:127`) and My Studio → Studio announcements (`StudioSection.tsx:359`) use the uid. On any account whose profile id is not its uid (the "older account" case CLAUDE.md flags as always-on), posting from one door works and from the other is refused. The codebase has **four idioms** for "who am I": `authTrainer.id` (23 sites), `auth.currentUser?.uid` (36), `uid || authTrainer.id` (`StudioSection.tsx:359`), and `uid ?? authTrainer.authUid ?? authTrainer.id` (`AdminDashboardView.tsx:302`, `OverviewPage.tsx:126`). *Direction:* one `currentActor()` helper returning `{ id: uid, name }`, and a lint rule against `authTrainer.id` as an author.

8. **Medium — `src/features/relay/jobs/JobComposer.tsx` (403 lines) and `relay/GlanceBand.tsx` (110 lines) are unreachable.** `JobComposer` opens only when `relay` is null (`StudioHubView.tsx:427`, `TeamPanel.tsx:136,239`); both hosts mount only inside `RelayProvider` (`MyStudioView.tsx:178`, the sole provider), so `setComposingJob`/`setComposing` never run. `GlanceBand` renders only when `embedded && !relay` (`StudioHubView.tsx:314-317`), and `StudioHubView` is mounted only embedded by `PlannerView.tsx:145` under the provider; its `glance` memo (`:283-295`) is computed on every render for nothing. The non-embedded "Studio hub" header (`:318-329`) is dead too. Invariant 11: nothing ships unreachable. *Direction:* delete the fallbacks and the composer; Capture is the composer.

9. **Medium — `src/features/trainer-profile/EditTrainerModal.tsx:669-859`.** A 191-line `isAdminMode` branch (role select offering Owner/Founder/Admin, home/accessible/guest studio checkboxes) plus eight `useState`s for it, and `isAdminMode` is never passed (`TrainerProfileView.tsx:173-180` is the only mount). Dead — and if it were ever enabled it would be a third role editor that ignores `mayHandOut`. The live half writes the whole payload, not the diff (`:257-269`: `mindbodyStaffId: ... || ""`, `photoUrl: photoUrl || null`, `email`), and `TrainerProfileView.tsx:99-106` still has a whole-object `setDoc` fallback. `mindbodyStaffId` is self-editable and is not in `writesAccessFields` (`firestore.rules:787-791`). *Direction:* cut the modal to bio/certifications/tenure/nickname on the admin kit; staff identity belongs to StaffEditor.
