# The fix round — what the iPad said about the tracker round

**Sep 13, 2026 · seven code patches + this document · branch `fix-round`**

AJ took the tracker round (`2026-09-13-tracker-round.md`) onto his iPad the same evening and reported seven things. Each is one commit, typechecked and tested on its own, so any one can be reverted alone. The "root cause" lines are written for the next person who has to touch the same code.

## What he reported, and what changed

| He saw | Root cause | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hub:** the marker chips (Session 25, Birthday tomorrow…) sit on top of the session time; it can't be read | The card is exactly as tall as its 30-minute slot.
