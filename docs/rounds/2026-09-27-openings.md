### Where this document differs from the sketch AJ approved

Three reviews found places where the sketch would have said something confidently wrong. Each change is explained again where it comes up.

1. **A week counts only when Journey read it in full from Mindbody.** Journey keeps no record today of which past days it read in full. A day can hold a handful of bookings that arrived one at a time, and it then looks just like a quiet day. So from this round on, each iPad writes down the days its pulls read in full (a small new record, **needs AJ's OK**). Until four weeks are recorded, the usual week says "not enough weeks yet". An optional one-off back-read from Operations → Mindbody can give it eight weeks on the first day (also **needs AJ's OK**).
2. **On Openings, "since" is the first week counted, not the cutover date.** Journey syncs bookings whether or not a studio has cut over.
3. **The Sunday job re-reads eight weeks each time**: about 2,000 to 2,500 small reads for a busy studio, not "a few hundred". It is still a fraction of a cent.
4. **An agreed standing week describes the days from the day it was agreed.** The grid doesn't apply today's weeks to the past. So the words about room start forming on the day leaders agree the weeks, and appear four weeks later. Until then the grid says how many are usually booked.
5. **Regulars come only from the agreed standing weeks.** "A regular" and "not a regular" are fixed in the trainer's standing week. The marks on Openings are about a time: "Always full" and "Usually has room".
6. **Other trainers' names are for leaders.** Everyone else sees times, "with you" and "1 trainer". Client names show only after a tap, so the screen can be turned to face a client.
7. **A time is offered "for good" only when the coming weeks on file are free** and no agreed regular sits there.
8. **Team's "The next seven days" moves to Openings**, so one rule feeds one list. The Operations Overview gets one line pointing to it.
9. **The Wrap-up's door opens a sheet on top of the Wrap-up, showing times only.** It shows quietly on every Wrap-up that has something to offer, and prominently when nothing is booked.
10. **"First session to last" splits at breaks.** It is the trainer's own; leaders see clients and session time.
11. **My clients has no "since" date,** says "in Journey", and puts the clients coached lately first.
12. **Three blocks a day on the standing week,** so "three clients, a little break" fits (a one-number rules change, **needs AJ's OK**).

## The shape, in one paragraph

**Openings** is a new section of My Studio for everyone who works at the studio. It answers three questions in sentences:

- **The usual week.** A Monday-to-Saturday grid of half-hours. Each one says in a word or two whether that time is usually full, usually has room, or neither, and a tap shows the proof. *"Monday 8:00 AM · Usually full: full in 7 of the last 8 Mondays."*
- **Next 7 days.** What opened up: a cancellation nobody rebooked, a regular who isn't booked, a usually-full time with room. *"Mon, Oct 5 · 8:00 AM · usually full · room with 1 trainer. A regular isn't booked this week; booked again from Mon, Oct 19."*
- **A new regular time.** Times that have room most weeks and are free on the coming weeks on file. *"Tuesday 10:30 AM · usually has room (6 of the last 8 Tuesdays), and free on the next 3 Tuesdays on file."*

Who is in comes from the agreed standing weeks, whose "hours" become "When I usually take clients". Who is booked comes from the Mindbody bookings Journey already syncs, counted only on days Journey read in full. Once a week, the existing Sunday job folds eight weeks into one small document per studio, so the screen reads one document instead of two months of bookings.

The Wrap-up offers the same times to the client standing there, as a sheet of times with no names. My Profile gains two cards:

- **Your week:** clients trained, session time, and first session to last each day.
- **My clients:** the clients you have trained most in Journey, beside the Kaizen Roster.

## What it never does

- **It books nothing, holds nothing, and asks Mindbody nothing new.** It reads the bookings Journey already syncs. The trainer or the front desk books in Mindbody, as today (ARCHITECTURE §1.7; Mindbody charges $2.50 for each appointment booked through its API). There is no new timer and no new Mindbody call. The record of whole reads is written after pulls that already happen.
- **It never promises a time.** Journey doesn't read a trainer's availability or time off in Mindbody. Every offer ends: "Check it in Mindbody before you promise it. Journey doesn't book."
- **It contacts nobody.** No bell, email, text or push. Openings is a screen a person opens.
- **It says "room" only on a day it can stand behind.** In the past, that is a day Journey read in full. In the next 7 days, it is a read the server answered. Otherwise it says "can't tell yet", as the standing week does.
- **It never totals, counts or ranks trainers' free time.** Lines run by time. A trainer sees their own room as "with you". Leaders see who has room at a time, so they can fill it, always in name order and never with a count beside a name.
- **It guesses no regulars.** A regular is one a trainer marked and a leader agreed.
- **Booked means booked, not attended.** Whether a client came is a separate question with its own answer (done means logged).
- **It never shows a client another client's name.** The Wrap-up's sheet shows times only. On Openings, client names show only after a tap.
- **Your week is not a timesheet.** It counts sessions and the time from the first session to the last, and the card says so. (AJ, Sep 19: "No payroll on the app for now.")
- **It is not on the Hub, and not on Relay.** The Hub runs today; Relay is for the trainer between clients. The Hub outline for a regular's usual time is not built: Openings is where the studio sees "Austin's 8:00 isn't coming this week".

**Where it sits** (ARCHITECTURE §1.9):

- **Tier:** Valuable, non-blocking.
- **Floor loop:** rank 4 (shift gaps) for Openings and My Profile. The Wrap-up door is at rank 2, so it must never slow the Wrap-up: its reads run in the background, and nothing waits for them.
- **Fence:** Mindbody, read only.

## The words, exactly

Every sentence is built from these definitions. The code is pure, in `src/features/openings/`. Each number in capitals is a named constant with its own test, and every one is AJ's to change.

### Times and bookings

**A time** is a half-hour of a weekday, Monday to Saturday, on the studio's clock. "Monday 8:00 AM" means 8:00 to 8:30 (`ROW_MINUTES = 30`, the slot a session is booked in). The grid shows the times from the earliest to the latest that anyone was booked or in during the weeks counted. Dates are the studio's Eastern day, and weeks run Monday to Sunday, as everywhere else. Times are always written with AM or PM ("6:00 AM"), because a studio day runs from 5:30 in the morning to 8:00 at night.

**A booking fills every time it overlaps**, from its start to its end. A 30-minute booking at 8:00 fills Monday 8:00. A rare one at 8:15 fills both 8:00 and 8:30.

| In the bookings | Counts as |
| --- | --- |
| A booking, as Journey last saw it | Booked, for its trainer |
| Moved | Booked at the time it moved to |
| Cancelled more than 24 hours before its start (`LATE_CANCEL_HOURS`) | Not booked: the time had room in the end. Counted as a cancellation on the time's sheet |
| Cancelled less than 24 hours before its start | Still booked for the usual word, because by then the time couldn't be offered to anyone. Counted as a **late** cancellation on the time's sheet. In the next 7 days it is room, like any cancellation |
| Cancelled with no cancellation stamp (the old sweep before Sep 16, or any cancellation before stamps began on Sep 19) | Not booked, and not counted as a cancellation |
| Cancelled, with a stamp later than its own start (a back-read found it afterwards) | Not booked. Counted as a cancellation, never as a late one |
| On the studio rotation ("{studio} Rotation") | Booked, but for nobody in particular: it takes one free trainer's place without saying whose (AJ: rotation is context) |
| A booking Journey can't place with any trainer at the studio | Booked. Room that day can't be judged (below) |
| A Mindbody "Unavailable" block (a row whose client name says "Unavailable") | Not a booking. The Calendar and the Hub already treat these as blocked time. Nothing in the sync writes them on purpose, so the report script counts them before anything else relies on them |

**Whose booking it is** follows the standing week check's own rule, so Openings and the check can't disagree. First the trainer's id, when the sync matched the staff member to a Journey trainer. Then the Mindbody staff id, when the trainer's Mindbody link is on the same site. Then the same staff name. A name that differs proves nothing. This is `trainerOf` in `standing-week/check.ts`, exported and reused, not copied.

### Which days count

A past day counts only when both of these hold.

1. **Journey read it in full.** Mindbody's answer for that day came back whole on the day before, on the day itself, or any time after. From this round on, each iPad writes down the days its pulls read in full (see the data, part 1):
   - the 30-minute pull and the morning month pull cover today and tomorrow;
   - the header's Refresh covers today and tomorrow too;
   - a back-read from Operations → Mindbody covers the past days it asks for.

   A day with no such record doesn't count, however many bookings it holds.
2. **The studio was open.** Its bookings are at least a quarter of that weekday's usual (`DAY_OPEN_SHARE = 0.25` of the median of the counted days of that weekday in the eight weeks). A day below that reads as closed, or nearly (a holiday, a snow day).

A day that fails is left out of every count, and each time's sheet names it:

- *"Not counted: Mon, Oct 12. Journey didn't read it in full."*
- *"Not counted: Thu, Nov 26, 2 bookings (a Thursday usually has 38). Closed, or nearly."*

**Why not a volume test alone** (the first draft's). It compared each day with the median of the same history. Before launch, no iPad pulls every day, and the webhook (on since Sep 23) brings only bookings made or changed since then; a standing booking made months ago reaches Journey only through a pull. So every week may hold only part of its bookings. Then every day passes a test against its own weekday, and the grid calls a standing hot spot "Usually has room": the exact opposite of the truth. Only a record made at the time of the read can tell a whole day from a partial one.

Until the record exists, no day counts. The usual week starts from nothing on the day this ships, unless AJ chooses the back-read (the data, part 1).

The Demo studio is the exception: its bookings are written by the seeder, so every Demo day holding a demo booking counts; the weeks before a seed or a Reset don't (see Demo Mode).

### Who is in

**On a past day**, a trainer is **in** at a time when both hold:

- **An agreed week was in force that day** and has them taking clients across that whole half-hour. An agreed week is in force from the day a leader agreed it until the day it was changed. The Sunday job keeps the older versions for eight weeks (the data, part 2), so a changed week keeps its history. A day before a trainer's first agreement has no agreed week for them. Today's weeks are never applied to the past: a trainer who used to start at 6:00 and now starts at 8:00 would otherwise turn every old 6:00 into "nobody in" and every new 12:30 into "room".
- **They had at least one booking at the studio that day.** A trainer with no booking all day may not have worked, and Journey can't tell. Past days away drop off the standing week once they pass, so this is also what keeps an old vacation from reading as room.

**In the next 7 days**, a trainer is in when their agreed week has them in then, they still work at the studio (`src/lib/who-works-here.ts`), and they haven't marked the day away. A day that hasn't happened can't prove more, so every line about room ahead says "usually takes clients then".

### Whether a day's room can be judged

A past counted day's room can be judged only when **everyone who had a booking there that day is known**:

- every trainer with a booking that day had an agreed week in force that day; and
- no booking that day is one Journey couldn't place with a trainer.

Rotation bookings don't stop a day being judged.

**Why.** Take Kim, whose week isn't agreed, with bookings at 7:00 and 9:00. She was probably in and free at 8:00, but Journey can't know it. If only Sam counted, 8:00 would read "Full" when Kim had room, and eight such weeks would make a false "Always full". So until everyone in the day is known, the day's times say how many were booked, never whether there was room. The rule is the same for "full" and "room", so neither word is favoured.

### One time, in one counted week

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Full** | The day can be judged; at least one trainer is in; every trainer in is booked then, once each rotation booking has taken a free trainer's place |
| **Room** | The day can be judged, and at least one trainer in is left with no booking |
| **Nobody booked** | Room, with no booking at all at that time |
| **Nobody in** | No trainer is in. If there are bookings anyway (a rotation Saturday, say), the time shows how many were booked |
| **Booked only** | The day can't be judged. The time says how many were booked and nothing about room |

### The usual word for a time

A time's **counted weeks** are the weeks, at most the last 8 (`WINDOW_WEEKS`), in which its weekday counted. Its **judged weeks** are the counted weeks whose day could also be judged.

| Word | Exactly | Example sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Always full** | Full in every judged week, with at least 6 judged (`ALWAYS_MIN_WEEKS`) | "Monday 8:00 AM · Always full: full in all of the last 8 Mondays." |
| **Usually full** (a hot spot) | Full in at least 3 of every 4 judged weeks (`USUAL_SHARE = 0.75`), with at least 4 judged (`MIN_WEEKS`) | "Monday 8:30 AM · Usually full: full in 7 of the last 8 Mondays." |
| **Usually has room** (a cold spot) | Room in at least 3 of every 4 judged weeks, with at least 4 judged | "Tuesday 10:30 AM · Usually has room: room in 6 of the last 8 Tuesdays, and nobody booked in 4 of them." |
| **Mixed** | At least 4 judged, and neither of the above | "Thursday 5:00 PM · Mixed: full in 4 of the last 8 Thursdays." |
| **N booked** | Fewer than 4 judged, at least 4 counted, and bookings | "Wednesday 6:00 AM · Usually 3 booked (3 or more in 6 of the 7 Wednesdays counted). Room can't be judged yet: not every trainer's week is agreed." |
| **Rotation** | Bookings on the rotation, and nobody in | "Saturday 9:00 AM · Usually 5 booked on the rotation. Saturdays run on the rotation: ask the front desk." |
| **–** (not enough weeks) | Fewer than 4 counted | "Friday 7:00 PM · Not enough weeks yet: 3 Fridays counted so far." |
| (blank) | Nothing booked and nobody in, in every counted week | (nothing) |

- "Usually N booked" is the largest number reached in at least 3 of every 4 counted weeks. It is the grid's hot and cold by demand, and it works before any week is agreed.
- A sentence says "of the last 8 Mondays" when all 8 counted, and "of the 6 Mondays counted" when fewer did.
- AJ's example, "booked in 7 of the last 8 Mondays", reads "Usually full" here, with "7 of 8" in its sentence. "Always" is kept for every single week, so an "always" never has an exception.

**The time's sheet** (a tap on the time) also says:

- the days not counted or not judged, and why;
- how many are usually booked, and how many are usually in (by name for leaders; see "Who sees names");
- how many **regulars** today's agreed weeks put at that time ("4 regulars at Monday 8:00 on the agreed weeks"). A regular matches a time within 15 minutes (`SLOT_TOLERANCE_MINUTES`, the standing week's own);
- the time's cancellations: *"Cancelled in 3 of the last 8 Mondays (2 of them late)."* Only stamped cancellations count, and a stamp later than the start is never late. This is AJ's "something that looks at our cancellations";
- when the bookings regularly outnumber the trainers in: *"More booked than the agreed weeks have in, in 6 of the last 8 Mondays. Someone's usual week may be missing this time."* This is the nearest the grid can come to AJ's "too many people going at the same time" without a floor limit (see the open questions);
- any mark on the time, and "Mark this time".

**Before any week is agreed**, every time shows its "N booked" form, and the top of the grid says why: *"Who's in comes from the standing weeks leaders agree on Team. None is agreed at Westlake yet, so this shows how many are usually booked, not whether there's room."* After the weeks are agreed, the room words form over the next four weeks read in full. Leaders also see who is still waiting: *"Kim's week isn't agreed yet."* Everyone else sees *"One trainer's week isn't agreed yet."* The missing step is usually the leader's agreement, so no trainer is named in front of colleagues.

### Next 7 days

**Next 7 days** covers today and the six studio days after it, the same window as the standing week check. It is read live, the way Team reads it today: `useWeekSchedule` with `{ confirmed: true }`, and the wait for the server's answer. It lists **one line per day and time still ahead**, in time order. A time gets a line when any of these is true:

1. **A cancellation that nobody rebooked.** A stamped cancellation for that time, with no real rebook after it. A real rebook is judged with `isRealRebook`, the one rule that Changes, the client calendar and the check all read. This line needs no agreed week, so it works from the first day: *"Thu, Oct 8 · 5:30 PM · usually 4 booked · a cancellation on Oct 2, not rebooked (3 booked now)."* The "usually 4 booked" part appears once the usual week knows the time. This is AJ's "one of our 8 a.m.s on Monday just opened up".
2. **A regular who isn't booked.** The standing week check's "open" and "moved" findings (`check.ts`). These are the free slots Team shows today (see "Team and the Overview").
3. **A usually-full time with room.** The time's usual word is Always full or Usually full (or it is marked "Always full"), and a trainer who is in has no booking then.

Each line gives one reason: the first of these that Journey can prove.

- The check found the regular not booked, or booked somewhere else: "A regular isn't booked this week." / "A regular is booked Tue, Oct 6 at 9:30 AM instead."
- A cancellation with no real rebook: "A cancellation on Oct 2, not rebooked."
- Otherwise: "Nothing is booked with them yet."

A regular's cancelled booking is both a check finding and a cancellation. It is said once: the two are matched by client, day, and time within 15 minutes.

**Room ahead** comes from the agreed weeks and the days away, so it is worded as what Journey knows: "room with 1 trainer" means a trainer usually takes clients then and has nothing booked. The line ends, on tap: "Check it in Mindbody before you promise it."

**When it can't tell.** While the read is loading, has failed, is offline or came only from the iPad's cache, the whole list says *"Can't tell yet. The next 7 days' bookings haven't come back from the server"*, exactly as Team does. A studio whose Mindbody isn't linked says so and lists nothing. With no agreed week yet, it says *"No standing week is agreed yet, so this lists cancellations only."*

### "Booked again from"

For every line that concerns a client, Journey makes one read of those clients' bookings from the day after the slot to **today + 30 days** at most (`clientId in [...]`, up to 30 clients at once, on the existing (clientId, startTime) index). It keeps only this studio's rows and drops cancelled ones.

| It says | When |
| --- | --- |
| "Booked again from Mon, Oct 19." | Her next booking is found, and every day before it was read: it falls inside the next 7 days, or the month was read in full today |
| "Next booking on file: Mon, Oct 19." | Her next booking is found, but the days before it weren't all read, so an earlier one could be missing |
| "Not booked in the next 30 days." | Nothing found, and the month was read in full today |
| "Not booked in the next 7 days; can't tell after that yet." | Nothing found, otherwise |
| "Can't tell yet." | The read hasn't come back from the server |

"The month was read in full today" means the sync lease's `lastDeepScheduleSyncAt` falls in today's pull block: the same test the auto-sync uses to decide whether to pull the month (`wantsDeepPull`). Journey holds bookings 30 days ahead only after that morning pull.

The line itself carries no client name: *"A regular isn't booked this week; booked again from Mon, Oct 19."* That is what a trainer needs to say AJ's "you can take that slot for the week, but he'll probably be back in two weeks".

### A new regular time

This part lists the times whose usual word is **Usually has room**, and any time marked "Usually has room". A time that reads or is marked **Always full** is never listed. Each listed time is then checked against today:

- **An agreed regular.** For a trainer whose agreed week has a regular within 15 minutes of the time, the time isn't theirs to offer. Under "Anyone", a time is left out when every trainer usually in then has a regular there.
- **The coming weeks.** When the month was read in full today, the next 3 occurrences after this week (`OFFER_AHEAD_WEEKS = 3`) are checked against the bookings on file. A time is listed only when it is free on all three, because AJ's offer is "we could just take that for good for you". When the month wasn't read in full today, the time is listed with *"Can't check the coming Tuesdays yet."*
- **This week** beside it: "This Tuesday, Oct 6: room" or "This Tuesday: full".

The sentence: *"Tuesday 10:30 AM · usually has room: room in 6 of the last 8 Tuesdays, and free on the next 3 Tuesdays on file."* The foot of the list: *"Check it in Mindbody before you promise it. Journey doesn't book."*

The coming weeks are read only when this part (or the Wrap-up's sheet) is opened: one read of the studio's bookings for days 7 to 27, on the existing (studioId, startTime) index, answered by the server.

### Who sees names

Openings is open to everyone who works at the studio, and a trainer's free time is theirs, not a scoreboard. So the screen shows names like this:

| | Leaders (head trainer, studio leader, studio owner at that studio, the grant: `leadsHere`), franchise owners, administrators | Everyone else who works there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trainer names | Shown, in name order ("room with Sam", "Usually in: Pat, Sam") | "you" for themselves; "1 trainer", "2 trainers" for anyone else |
| Client names | After a tap on a line | After a tap on a line |
| Narrowing by trainer | "With Sam" chips, in name order, with no counts | "With you · Anyone" |
| Whose week isn't agreed | By name | "One trainer's week isn't agreed yet" |

Client names wait for a tap on every screen of Openings, so a trainer can turn the iPad to a client. The reason line without the tap says "a regular" or "a cancellation".

This is how the screen shows them, not a lock. Firestore rules apply to whole documents, and everyone at the studio can already read its bookings and agreed weeks ("schedules are open to all", AJ, Sep 27). A true lock would need separate documents, and none is proposed. **Needs AJ's OK** (listed below).

### Marks: how a person disagrees

A mark is a person's word on a time, in the grid's own words:

- **Always full** (AJ's "that spot's just always taken")
- **Usually has room**

It can carry a short note, and it always carries who marked it and when. A mark sits **beside** the numbers and never replaces them. The time shows the mark first ("Marked Always full by Jo, Oct 3"), then what the bookings say ("The bookings say: full in 5 of the last 8 Mondays"). When the bookings clearly disagree (room in at least 3 of every 4 judged weeks against an "Always full" mark, or the reverse), the sheet says so first: *"The bookings disagree: room in 6 of the last 8 Mondays."*

What a mark changes:

- **Always full** counts as usually full for Next 7 days, and is never offered as a new regular time.
- **Usually has room** is offered, with the mark and the numbers shown.

Who: **anyone who works at the studio** may set, change or remove a mark, always as themselves. This follows the notes rule, where any trainer may close a thread and any trainer may reopen it. There is one mark per time.

**Review:** after 60 days (`MARK_REVIEW_DAYS`, as notes do) the sheet asks *"Marked 64 days ago. Still true?"* with **Keep** (it signs the mark again, as the person keeping it, today) and **Remove**. The mark keeps working while it waits; nothing drops on its own.

There is no "closed then" mark. The studio's hours belong to My Studio → Studio, and a time nobody is in already shows blank.

"A regular" and "not a regular" are not marks. The sheet's door for those goes to the standing week: My Profile for the trainer themselves, Team's Review for a leader. That keeps one record of who the regulars are.

### Your week

- **Where:** the studio the iPad is in. The card is titled **Your week at Westlake**.
- **Weeks:** this week (Monday to today) and last week, Monday to Sunday, as in Operations → Insights → Hours.
- **Whose sessions:** completed sessions whose trainer is you, by Hours' rule (the session's `trainerId`), each placed on a day by `sessionDay`. The read is the one Hours makes, `fetchSessionsInRange` (the studio's sessions since last Monday), filtered to you in memory, so the numbers are the ones Hours shows for you by construction. The read must come from the server: an answer from the iPad's cache says "can't read".
- **Clients trained:** distinct clients. **Sessions:** the count.
- **Session time:** sessions × the studio's session length (`sessionMinutesOf`, set on My Studio → Studio → The studio's day): *"9 h (18 sessions × 30 min)"*.
- **First session to last** (the trainer's own view only): for each day, from the start of the first session to the end of the last, **split wherever sessions are more than 90 minutes apart** (`SPAN_BREAK_MINUTES`). *"Mon, Sep 28: 6:58 to 9:34 AM, and 4:02 to 7:10 PM (9 sessions)."* The parts are summed: *"24 h 10 min over 4 days"*. Only sessions with real clock times count, so it leaves out:
  - past sessions logged by hand (their times are placeholders);
  - imported sessions;
  - sessions still open;
  - any session longer than 180 minutes.

  A day with one session counts just that session.
- **The card says what it is:** *"Counts sessions logged in Journey at Westlake. First session to last isn't a timesheet: Journey doesn't know when you arrived or left."* And, on a tap: *"Coaching load above counts the nightly totals, which also count sessions you started for someone else and your practice in Demo Mode, so the two can differ."*
- **A failed read** says "Can't read the sessions just now", never zero. More than 1,500 sessions (the read's cap) says "Too many sessions to count here."

### My clients

- **Where the numbers come from:** the studio's client list the app already holds, keeping only clients whose home is this studio (the list also carries booked visitors, who are left out). Each client's `trainerTally` records how many sessions they have had with each trainer. No new read.
- **Order:** first, **Coached lately**: the clients the nightly renewal snapshot says you coached in the last 60 days (`renewal.coachIds`, the list Relay → Mine already uses), most sessions with you first, then by name. Then everyone else you have trained, behind "Show all N". The first 12 show.
- **Each row:** the name (never cut short); *"42 sessions with you in Journey"*; the last session, worded through `src/lib/history-claims.ts` like every sentence about a client's past: *"Last in Journey: Sep 25"* until her home studio's coverage is complete; and the Kaizen Roster's mark when she is on your list. A tap opens the client.
- **The card says what it counts:** *"Sessions with you on record in Journey. Past sessions logged by hand count; imported sessions count only where the import linked them to you. Clients whose home is another studio aren't listed."*
- While the client list is loading or has failed, the card says "Can't read the client list just now", never an empty list.

### 1. Which days Journey read in full: `studios/{studioId}/scheduleCoverage/{month}` (a new collection and a rules block, **needs AJ's OK**)

```
studios/{studioId}/scheduleCoverage/{yyyy-mm}        e.g. "2026-10"
  days   ["2026-10-01", "2026-10-02", ...]   studio days Journey read in full
                                              on the day before, the day, or after
```

- **Written by** the iPad that pulled, straight after a pull whose answer was whole (`windowComplete`, the test the lease already uses). It adds each day of the pull's range that is no later than tomorrow, with an add-to-list write, so two iPads never overwrite each other. The places that pull are `useAutoSync` (the morning month pull and the 30-minute pull), the header's Refresh, and Operations → Mindbody's Sync. Each iPad remembers what it has recorded, so it writes about once or twice a day per studio.
- **Nothing about the pull changes:** what Journey asks Mindbody, and how often, stays as it is.
- **Read by:** the Sunday job (which days count) and the report script.
- **Rules (new block):**
  - read: anyone signed in, as for the sync lease;
  - add: anyone who works at the studio (`writesForStudio`), including administrators running a back-read;
  - the id must be a month (`2026-10`), the only key is `days`, and `days` is a list of at most 31;
  - delete: administrators only.
- **Rules tests:** a trainer of the studio adds a day; someone from another studio is refused; a 32-entry list, a wrong id and an extra key are refused.
- **Index:** none (read by id).
- **It can ship first,** ahead of the rest of the round (see Deploy order), so the weeks start counting sooner.

**The optional back-read (needs AJ's OK).** Operations → Mindbody → Sync already accepts a past "Pull from" date. The Sep 16 round named it as the repair for the old sweep's damage. Run once per studio over the last eight weeks after part 1 is live, it records those days as read in full, and the usual week has eight weeks on its first Sunday instead of zero. What it does besides:

- **It asks Mindbody** for about two months of that studio's bookings once: roughly the calls of two morning month pulls. It is done by hand, not on a timer.
- **It puts back the bookings the old sweep marked cancelled.** Before a studio's cutover, the renewals job counts a past booking as a visit, so those clients' visit counts rise to what they really were.
- **A cancellation it finds for the first time is stamped with the day of the back-read.** Openings never reads such a stamp as late. Operations → Changes looks only at the week ahead, so it is unaffected.

### 2. The weekly summary: `studios/{studioId}/watch/openings` (a new document, **needs AJ's OK**)

```
studios/{studioId}/watch/openings        written only by the Sunday job
  v         1                         the version the screen checks
  builtAt   when the job built it     ("Built Sunday, Oct 4")
  tz        the studio clock the job used
  row       30                        minutes in a row
  since     the first Monday with a counted day, or null
  weeks     the last 8 Monday-to-Saturday weeks, newest first
              m    "2026-09-28"       the Monday
              d    Monday to Saturday: bookings on file, and whether the day
                   counted ("" counted, "r" not read in full, "c" closed or
                   nearly) and whether it could be judged
  who       short key -> { id: trainers/{id}, n: name }   every trainer a time names
  agreed    short key -> [ { from, to, blocks } ]         the agreed weeks in force,
                                                          kept 8 weeks back
  cells     "1-0800" -> { week index -> { s, b, r, c, l, i } }
              s  the word for that week: full, room, nobody booked,
                 nobody in, or booked only
              b  bookings (a late cancellation counts)
              r  of which on the rotation
              c  stamped cancellations
              l  of which late
              i  the trainers in (short keys)
```

- **No client names or ids.** Bookings are recorded by trainer only.
- **The words for each past week are worked out by the job,** with the same pure rule (`room.ts`) the screen uses for the next 7 days. The job places each booking with its trainer by the check's rule, reading the small trainers list.
- **`agreed`** keeps each agreed week's older versions. Each Sunday the job takes each current agreed week, in force from the day it was agreed (`finalAt`). Where last Sunday's document held a different version, that version is closed on the day before and kept. Versions older than eight weeks drop off. If last Sunday's document is missing or unreadable, older weeks use only the current version, from the day it was agreed; the grid then says less, never more.
- **Size:** kept terse (short keys, empty lists and zero counts left out, weeks as a map, never a list inside a list, which Firestore refuses). That is about 60 to 120 KiB for a busy studio, far under Firestore's 1 MiB. The fold's test asserts a ceiling of 256 KiB. The job skips a studio whose document would pass 512 KiB, with a log line, and keeps last week's.
- **One time key everywhere:** "1-0800" (weekday 1 to 6, then the half-hour), for cells and for marks alike.
- **Written by:** the Sunday job (the Admin SDK).
- **Read by:** My Studio → Openings (all three parts), the Wrap-up's sheet and its door, Team's line and the Overview's line.
- **Every field has a reader:**

| Field | Read by |
| --- | --- |
| `v` | the reader's guard |
| `builtAt` | the "Built Sunday, Oct 4" line; an old date shows as old |
| `tz`, `row` | `row`: the grid's rows. `tz`: no reader yet (corrected by the final review; the screens use the studio document's own clock, and "Left for a later round" has it) |
| `since` | the since line |
| `weeks` | every "N of M" count, the "Not counted" lines, the day's usual count for the closure test |
| `who` | the names in leaders' sentences, including someone who has left |
| `agreed` | next Sunday's job (the history of agreed weeks), and the sheet's "usually in" |
| `cells` | every word on the grid, the sheet's counts and cancellations |

- **Rules: no change.** `match /watch/{watchId}` already lets everyone who works at the studio read it (`writesForStudio`) and nothing in the app write it. A rules test adds the new name to the existing watch tests.
- **Index: none new.**

**Why fold on Sunday rather than read on the screen.** Eight weeks of a busy studio is about 2,400 bookings, about 1.4 MB on the studio's Wi-Fi every time someone opens Openings or the Wrap-up's sheet. "Keep running totals instead of re-reading history" is a house rule, and the Sunday job already writes a document per studio for the Overview (`watch/performance`).

**Why the Sunday job, not the nightly renewals job.** The renewals job already holds every studio's bookings from 91 days back to 31 ahead, so it would add no reads. But it calls Mindbody, it reads the whole bookings collection every night with no studio named (see "Seen on the way"), and a failure there would tie Openings to renewals. The usual week changes slowly; the parts that change daily (Next 7 days, the coming weeks) are read live.

**Why rebuild all eight weeks rather than add one.** It is cheap either way (see the cost table). Rebuilding means a change Mindbody sends later about a past week still reaches the summary, a missed Sunday heals itself the next week, and the bookings carry nothing from one run to the next that could drift. Only the agreed weeks' history is carried, because nothing else holds it.

### 4. A new step in the Sunday job (**needs AJ's OK**)

The step is step 8 of `server/machine-trends-job.ts`, after the performance watch.

- **Nothing about the service changes.** The Render service stays `journey-cron-leaderboards` (a renamed service would be a new service to Render). The schedule stays Sunday 07:00 UTC: 3 AM Eastern in summer and 2 AM in winter, when Monday to Saturday of the week just ended is complete.
- **What it reads** (it asks Mindbody nothing):
  - the studios: a handful of small documents (each studio's clock, whether its Mindbody is linked, whether it is the Demo studio). The job doesn't read these today;
  - the trainers: one small read of the fields the booking rule needs (id, name, Mindbody link);
  - for each studio whose bookings are linked (a Site ID and not marked offline; the Demo studio counts):
    - its bookings for the eight weeks (`studioId ==` plus a `startTime` range, on the existing index);
    - its whole-read record for the months those weeks touch (at most 3 small documents, by id);
    - its standing weeks (one small collection);
    - last Sunday's `watch/openings` (one document).
- **The window** is the eight Monday-to-Saturday weeks before the studio's current week, worked out from the studio's today in its own time zone. It is never "now is Sunday", never UTC days (the machine-trends window counts UTC days and must not be reused), and never the time zone of the computer running it.
- **What it writes:** one `watch/openings` per studio. Each is built on its own inside the step, cleaned of `undefined` (the Admin SDK refuses it, as the app does) and size-checked. A studio that fails is skipped with a log line, and the others go ahead.
- **Its own commit.** The Openings documents are written in their own batch **after** the job's main write, inside their own try/catch. Today every step's writes go out together, outside the steps' own catches, so a bad document would take the trends, the Kaizen reports and the performance watch down with it. With its own commit, nothing the Openings step does can touch them, and last week's Openings documents stay in place.
- **Two scripts:**
  - `scripts/run-machine-trends.ts --only openings` runs just this step (dry run by default, `--commit` to write), so seeding Openings never re-runs the whole weekly job;
  - `scripts/openings-report.ts` (new, always read-only) prints, per studio and per week: the bookings on file, which days counted and why not, how many rows came only through the webhook (they carry its event stamp) against rows a pull saw, and how many "Unavailable" rows there are. AJ can check one studio against Mindbody's own report before trusting any word.

### The Wrap-up

**The Next card's check.**

- A booking found in the schedule already loaded is shown at once, as today.
- Otherwise the card listens to the client's own bookings from now on (the query the profile header makes, up to 50 rows, cancelled ones dropped), answered by the server. It is a listener, not a single read: if the desk books her while she is still standing there, the webhook writes the booking within seconds, the card turns green, and the door steps back.
- A booking at another studio counts where Mindbody knows her by the same record. Westlake, Strongsville and Willoughby share one Mindbody, so a booking at any of the three shows: *"Next session: Tue, Oct 6 · 8:00 AM at Strongsville."* Solon keeps its own records, so a Solon booking of a Westlake client isn't seen.
- **Nothing on file**, confirmed by the server: never a plain "Nothing booked yet", because that is the strongest claim and it was made exactly when Journey had read least. Instead:
  - *"Nothing booked in the next 30 days. Book the next one before they leave."* when the month was read in full today;
  - *"Nothing booked in the next 7 days. Book the next one before they leave."* otherwise.
- **While waiting:** "Checking the next booking…" in space already kept for the answer, so nothing on the card moves while the client reads it. **Offline or failed:** "Can't check the next booking right now."
- Today the card judges "nothing booked" from about eight days of this studio's schedule, so a client booked ten days out, or at Strongsville, reads as unbooked. That is fixed here.

**The door: Times with room.**

**Rules the proposal left open, decided in the core** (each written down in `src/features/openings/README.md`):

- Exactly 24 hours before the start is on time; late is less than 24 (`LATE_CANCEL_HOURS`), as in "at least 24 hours' notice". A cancellation stamped at or after the booking's own start is a cancellation, never a late one.
- The summary's window is the eight Monday-to-Saturday weeks that have **ended** by the studio's today, so on Sunday it includes the week just ended. `since` is carried from last Sunday when it is older.

- A booking is read as lasting at most 180 minutes, and never past midnight (`LONGEST_BOOKING_MINUTES`, new).
- In the days ahead, room is judged per half-hour: only a booking Journey can't place at that very time stops "room".

- A week that is gone is closed on the day last Sunday's summary was built, and the same blocks agreed again keep their older start.

## Deploy order, as built

`scripts/ship/ship-openings.ps1` does steps 1 to 4 and prints steps 5 and 6.

1. **No index change.** The prepare stage checks that `firestore.indexes.json` is master's. If `firebase deploy --only firestore:indexes` is ever run and asks whether to delete indexes that are not in the file, **the answer is N**.
2. **`npm run test:rules`** on AJ's PC, the run that counts (202). The script stops an emulator left on port 8080 by an earlier run only when AJ types STOP, and stops the one its own run leaves behind.
3. **`firebase deploy --only firestore:rules --project prod`**: the marks block and the standing week's 21 blocks. They only add access, so the running app is unaffected, and **they must go before the app**: without them the marks read is refused (A new regular time and the Wrap-up's sheet offer nothing, and the Overview can't use the marks), and a standing week with a 15th block is refused. The restore tag `restore/2026-09-28-before-openings` (master as it is before the push) is pushed first.
4. **Push `openings` to `master`, fast-forward only. Render deploys the app**, and the next Sunday run of `journey-cron-leaderboards` (Sunday Oct 4, 07:00 UTC, 3 AM Eastern) builds from master and runs step 8.
5. **Before anyone saves a standing week with three blocks on a day, every iPad loads the new version** (go to the Hub, or tap the new-version line under the header). An iPad still on an older build keeps only the first 14 blocks, Monday first, and a leader agreeing a week on it saves the shortened week to both the proposal and the agreed week without a word. The repair: the trainer enters the blocks again on the new build, and a leader agrees it again.
6. **Optional: the first summary now**, so Openings doesn't wait for Sunday. In PowerShell, in the project folder (they need `service-account.json`):
   - `npx tsx scripts/openings-report.ts` (read-only: how many weeks each studio has counted);
