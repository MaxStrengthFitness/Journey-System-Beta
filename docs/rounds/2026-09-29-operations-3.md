# The Operations room, wave 3 — the case form, and "didn't come" read everywhere it should be

*Sep 29 2026. Branch `redesign/operations-3`, three commits on master's `bb90d773`, then this document. The second wave's leftovers (`docs/rounds/2026-09-28-operations-2.md`, "Not built"): item 6, the case form, and the three readers of a leader's "didn't come" mark that weren't the second wave's folders.*

## Built

1. **The case form** (`dfae1be4`). On the client page inside Operations (`shell/ClientPage.tsx` → `journey/JourneyCase.tsx`), under her case: a leader opens a case and changes its **owner** (everyone who works at the studio, `lib/who-works-here.ts`, stored by sign-in uid — `authUid`, else the trainer id for an account keyed on it), **next step**, **due day**, **outcome** (Open · Booked again · Paused · Lost) and **reason**; the case's owner changes their own four (never the owner); everyone else reads. `journey/case-form.ts` is the pure half (`caseRights` mirrors the rules so the screen never offers what the database refuses; `ownerChoices`; `startingDraft`: her usual trainer, the rules' due day, open, an EMPTY step, because an empty step reads the rules' own everywhere). `journey/CaseForm.tsx` is the admin kit's form: `useDirtyForm` (so the leave warning covers typed text), only the diff written through the existing `saveCase` / `casePatch`, a new case whole through the existing `openCase`, a Save bar that never blocks, 40px controls, admin tokens. Nothing deletes a case: an outcome closes it, and a leader setting it back to Open reopens it (`openedAt` kept, which the rules allow). A trainer who owns a case can't read the studio's `cases` collection, so `useClientCase` (new in `case-store.ts`, one document listener) reads their one document and `JourneyCase` works her case out again from it. The store's shape, the rules and Relay's query are untouched.
2. **The Hub reads "didn't come"** (`65e88961`). `ClientsView` reads `studios/{s}/bookingMarks` with ONE listener for the day on screen (`useBookingMarks`, for someone who works at the studio; never per card, never per client) and hands the marks to every card (`HubCard`'s `noShows`), the Next 30 minutes strip and the day's moments engine (`hub-opportunities/moments-today.ts`, `use-day-moments.ts`). `lib/hub-card-state.ts` gains `didnt-come`: the card recedes with a quiet **Didn't come**, never "Not logged"; the Opportunities list's state word is "didn't come". A session logged that day still beats the mark (done means logged); marks unread or refused change nothing. The card now passes the booking's id into `hubCardState`, which a mark is keyed by. Mindbody's own No-Show, should the sync ever carry it, reads the same word (it read as "past" and said nothing before).
3. **The client calendar draws "didn't come"** (`9e671c2a`). The History tab (`client-history/ClientHistoryTab.tsx`) reads the marks once, from the first day drawn to today, for the studio the iPad is in, and `bookings.ts` draws a marked past booking as a fourth mark kind, `didnt-come`: a ring in the day's corner, "Sep 22 · didn't come" under the month, "Didn't come" in the legend only when one is drawn, and the day's spoken label reads it. A mark is applied only to a booking at the marks' studio (`marksStudioId`), because the two Mindbody sites number appointments on their own. A session logged that day still beats it.

## Not built, and why

- **My Profile → Your week** reads no bookings: it is Hours' own rule over the trainer's completed sessions (`trainer-profile/your-week.ts`), so there is no "not logged" on it to become "didn't come". The only booking reader on My Profile is Today's schedule (`adapters.ts` `upcomingFor`), which lists bookings still to come — a marked booking's slot is already over, so it had already left the list. Nothing to change; if AJ wants Your week to say "12 booked · 1 didn't come", that is a bookings read the card doesn't make today, and a separate ask.
- **Reopening with a new owner and a fresh `openedAt`** (the store's header describes it) is not a separate button: a leader changes the owner and sets the outcome back to Open in the same save, `openedAt` kept. Both are what the rules allow; the fresh-`openedAt` variant can be added as a "Reopen" button if the case's age should restart.

## Open for AJ (defaults applied)

1. **Who may own a case**: everyone who works at the studio (home, also works at, a guest; active, not a placeholder). *Default: yes.*
2. **A new case's step starts empty** and reads the rules' own step until someone writes one. *Default: yes.*
3. **"Didn't come" is said on the floor**, as quietly as "Not logged" — a trainer sees that a leader marked a client's booking. *Default: yes* (the marks are readable by everyone who works there already, wave 2's rules).
4. **On the client calendar, a "didn't come" is a mark like a cancellation**: a ring on the day, a line under the month, in the legend only when drawn. *Default: yes.*
