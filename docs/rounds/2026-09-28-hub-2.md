## What was built

**1 — Get to know, switched on** (`4afde2e`). The ✎ "Ask about" from FORD (`hub-opportunities/get-to-know.ts`, the cherry round's rule, unchanged): a detail whose day comes round within a week of the booking's day, or one noted in the two weeks before it; never archived, the team's In one line, a legacy row, a detail marked no longer true or one whose window has closed; one per client, the soonest dated one first.

- *The read* (`ford/hub-read.ts`, the third FORD query shape, beside the two in `useClientFord.ts`). ONE collection group query, the Delight queue's kind, scoped by the studio, asking for exactly the three reasons a detail can come up on any day of the Hub's strip (today and the six after it): `studioId == S` and (`recurrence == "annual"`, whatever year it was stored in · `eventDate` inside the strip's two weeks · `occurredAt` in the last two weeks). It never reads the studio's whole FORD. One `getDocs`, capped at 1,000, no listener.
- *Once per studio visit* (`hub-opportunities/use-hub-ford.ts`). The first time the Hub opens on a studio, it reads; coming back to the Hub between sessions reuses the answer; flipping days never reads again (the window covers the whole strip). Another studio, a new studio day or a sign-out (`forgetOnSignOut`) reads again, and a read that lands after a sign-out is never kept for the next person. Only the server's answer is held: a failure, or an answer this iPad's cache gave alone, is asked again at the next visit or when the iPad comes back online. A detail noted today is asked about from the next visit.
- *Who reads it*: only someone the rules let read the studio's FORD — the people who work there, franchise owners and administrators — asked once in ClientsView (`readsStudio`, the app's mirror of `writesForStudio`, the answer the standing weeks already asked). A client visiting from another studio has her FORD at her home studio, so she is not in it and nothing is said.
- *The engine* (`moments-today.ts`): a sixth family, **get-to-know** (kind `ask-about`), last in the Key's order, handed in as `fordFor` the way the Critical notes are. A client whose FORD couldn't be checked is `askUnknown` — never "nothing to ask about".
- *The card*: the ✎ (a blue speech bubble, the Key's) ALONE, inside the two-glyph rule — on a card with two other marks it is part of the "+N". No word beside it in anyone's column, your own included, and its label is only "Something to ask about", so a client's home life is never on the grid, not even in a screen reader's label. A finished card drops it with every other mark.
- *The peek*: its sentence last among the lines — "Ask about: Her grandson's wedding in Hobbiton on Saturday — Saturday, Oct 3 (Family, noted Sep 15)." — and, when the studio's FORD couldn't be checked, a quiet line at its foot.
- *The Opportunities list*: the chip ("Ask: the recital · Sat"), the **Get to know** filter with its count, the sentence under "Something to say" in the opened row; when FORD couldn't be checked, one line under the chips and a quiet line in the row instead of "Nothing special today."
- *The top and the Key*: the day summary's chips are the list's own, so a sixth appears when anyone has something to ask about ("Get to know 2"; zero is never drawn); it lights its cards ("Showing 2 to ask about on the grid"). The Key gains its row.

**2 — All stars, switched on** (`c88374d`). AJ's Hub question 6: "the clients that come 2 times a week almost every week of the year for over 6 months". The iPad never counts the 26 weeks.

- *The read* (`hub-opportunities/use-hub-marks.ts`): ONE `getDoc` of `studios/{studioId}/watch/hubMarks` — `allStars: [{ clientId, weeksWithVisit, perWeek }]` and `computedAt` — written each night by the renewals job (the Operations helper's addition tonight). Held once per studio visit exactly as the FORD read is; a "there is none" from the server is held until the next studio day.
- *What may be said* (`all-stars.ts`, `readHubMarks`): nothing when the document is missing, older than three days (judged on the Hub's own clock, so marks that turn stale while it is open fall silent) or not one this app can read (no date, no list, a date more than a day ahead); a row the rule wouldn't stand behind — under 24 of 26 weeks, under about twice a week, no client — is dropped, never guessed at; nothing about a client the marks don't name, and nothing ever says who isn't one.
- *Where it shows*, as the cherry round designed: an **All stars** section after New · Building · Regulars on the Sessions sort, its rows "#264 · in 25 of the last 26 weeks" (a milestone today keeps its own section; a total that can't be quoted doesn't stop it, because the job's claim is about the 26 weeks Journey holds); and "All star: in 25 of the last 26 weeks, about twice a week." in the peek (one sentence under the marks) and in the opened row under "Where she is". Never on the card, never a chip or a filter.
- *Expect an empty list for months.* The rule holds only where Journey holds every visit in the 26 weeks (`allStarStanding`'s migration check: the part of her timeline Journey owns must reach back past the window's first day), and Journey has held the studios' visits only since this autumn — so a job that applies the rule as written names nobody until a studio has had about half a year on Journey. The section and the sentence then appear by themselves. If names appear sooner, the job is not applying that check, and that is the job's to fix: the Hub says whatever the job says.

## Reads

Two new reads on the Hub, each once per studio visit, each only for someone who works at the studio (or a franchise owner or an administrator):

| Read | Shape | Index | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The studio's FORD, for Get to know | ONE collection group query on `ford`: `studioId ==` and an OR of `recurrence == "annual"` · `eventDate` in a range · `occurredAt >=`; `limit(1000)`; one `getDocs` | `ford` collection group (studioId ↑, recurrence ↑) and (studioId ↑, occurredAt ↑) — **new**; (studioId ↑, eventDate ↑) — existing | None: the ford block's read (`isTrainerOfStudio(resource.data.studioId)`, franchise owners, administrators) already covers it |
| The nightly marks, for All stars | ONE `getDoc` by id: `studios/{s}/watch/hubMarks` | None (a read by id) | None added here: the existing `studios/{s}/watch/{watchId}` read (`writesForStudio`) covers it, and nobody in the app may write it |

No per-client query, no listener, no write, no Mindbody call, no Cloud Function, no server change, no new stored field.

## How to review it on an iPad

The checklist round is in "For the integrator" below. All stars will show nothing until the renewals job names someone.

## Left for later (technical)

- If the emulator (or the Enterprise edition) ever refuses the FORD read's `or()`, the fallback is three plain queries with the same three indexes; `ford/hub-read.ts` is the only place that would change.
- The FORD read's guard rail (1,000) makes the answer partial; a studio that reaches it should get a tighter read (the `eventDate` branch is the widest: every annual day at the studio).
- Surgery or away from dated notes (from the calm Hub's list): its FORD half could ride on this read; the dated notes need a journal read of their own.
