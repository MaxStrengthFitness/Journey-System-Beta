## The waiver (item 10): what was found

The card flags only a **definite** "not signed" (`isLiabilityReleased === false`); a missing field was already "Not synced yet" and flagged nothing. Journey never writes that field itself. Its only writers are Mindbody's own answer: Master Sync's `Liability.IsReleased` (`lib/mindbody-master-patch.ts`) and the webhook's `client.updated` (`functions/src/mindbody/index.ts`). So 32 of 32 is Mindbody saying its liability release was never ticked for those clients, which is what a studio that keeps its waivers on paper (or anywhere but Mindbody) looks like.

**Not confirmed against the data**: a read-only count of Strongsville's clients' waiver field was not run from this session. AJ can confirm it in Mindbody: open two or three of those clients and look at their liability release.

The reading now: with no client the Hub holds for the studio signed in Mindbody, "not signed" is unknown on the Hub and no card is flagged; where some are signed, the ones who aren't are still flagged. The profile's Account still says exactly what Mindbody says.
