# The colour round: the Navy Frame — Oct 4 2026

Branch `colour/navy-frame`, off master `db46d32c`. One commit per phase, each typechecked at the baseline (2) and run through the full suite, then one commit of the review's fixes and this one of docs. The phases are separate so each can be reverted alone; they ship as ONE push, because a half-moved palette looks patched. No Firestore rules, indexes or functions change.

## What AJ asked

Light mode was "so bright ... like a negative mode", dark mode was "so gray", and "in the hub, nothing has color". Use the Max Strength logo's colours: the blue `#0A548B`, the orange `#F36D21` and the slate `#5B6770`.

The palettes were measured and drawn on the real Hub first (four options and a runner-up; the working files, renders and checks are in `harness/colour/` on AJ's PC, which git ignores). The pick is the **Navy Frame**.

## AJ's three answers

"ill take your pick for all 3 questions":

1. **1A — the frame is the logo's navy in both modes.** The header, the bottom bar and the iPad status bar are `--chrome` `#002341`, light and dark alike.
2. **2A — a client card still to come gets a quiet blue left edge** on the Hub (`--eq-rail-booked`), so the Hub has colour before the first session of the day.
3. **3A — the studio "Accent colour" is removed.** `ActiveStudioContext` wrote the studio's `brandColor` into `--cta` on every screen, so no new orange could ever show; the effect and its Admins control are gone. Stored `brandColor` values are left as they are (no script, no write). The trainer's own `brandColor` (their avatar) is a different field and stays.

## What it looks like now

- **Light mode is calm daylight:** off-white cards `#F3F6F9` on a soft grey-blue ground `#DEE6EE`, navy ink `#192D41` (13:1; it was 17-18:1 on pure white). Nothing is pure white any more.
- **Dark mode is studio navy:** ground `#0A1C2C`, cards one step up `#14293D`, soft ink `#DFE7EF` (12:1). Every `slate-*` class follows, because the dark neutral ramp `--n-*` is now the navy ramp, not Tailwind slate. **This reverses the Sep 12 2026 decision "dark must not move at all"** (VISUAL-CONSISTENCY-ROUND), which is why dark used to be a navy shell over near-black content.
- **Blue** (the logo blue, lifted to `#65ABE9` in dark) means yours, picked, or in session.
- **Orange means now and go, in two tokens.** `--eq-hero` is the orange of marks: the now line, its dot and pill, today's ring, the day dots (`#D45A06` in light, the logo orange's hue, as deep as 3:1 on the grid needs; the logo orange in dark). `--eq-go` is the one loud action (Start session, Finish) and every orange chip with words: the true logo orange with **navy words** (6.05:1), both modes. **No white words sit on any orange** (white on the logo orange is 2.99:1).
- **Crimson** keeps exactly `#C0203F` / `#F2718C`: Critical, destructive and rep quality only. Plum stays caution.
- **Dark fills are opaque and keep their own hue.** An rgba orange or crimson wash over navy cancels to grey or violet (the 16% hero wash came out `#383439`, chroma 0.010, and Critical and caution were 2.2 CIEDE2000 apart). Each dark fill is now an opaque colour at its accent's hue: hero `#4b2915`, alert `#472024`, warn `#402846`, ok `#113e36`, live `#23405c`.

## Values that differ from the plan, and why

- `--chrome-here` is `#65ABE9` (dark `--primary`), not `#72B8F2`: two near-identical light blues on one dark screen read as an accident. 6.5:1 on the frame.
- Light `--eq-ink-faint` is `#7f8c99`, not `#84919e`, so a card's default left edge stays 3:1 on the new card. Every copy follows.
- **Light ok green is `#17714b`** (the review): the plan kept `#1c7a52`, but the grounds under it darkened, so "Saved" in every Operations and Admins save bar and a done card on the Board fell to 4.42:1. Lightness only; 4.8:1 on the page, 5.0 on surface-2, 5.5 on a card, and white on it 6.0. It stays apart from crimson for a deuteranope (CIE76 20.4).
- The Active Session's dark Today column is `#143a5c`, not `#1e4466`: the plan's value put the muted words on it at 4.41:1. It stands 1.27:1 off the cells.
- The session grid's light `--jg-border-strong` is the softer `#a3aebb` (2.08:1), so its sticky edges stay decorative; every other palette uses the Hub's 3:1 `#7a8694`.
- **Light "done" against an empty grid cell falls from 1.16 to 1.07.** It can't be restored without moving the max and poor fills, whose lightness carries rep quality; the weight and reps in the cell still say a set happened.
- A finished Hub card recedes at 0.70 (was 0.62), so its name reads (4.96:1 light), and its quiet words lift to the ink (the review).
- Pulse's light yellow is `#9d5f00` (4.76 on the card), the calendar's light heat step 4 `#2b7bb0` (white on it 4.60), the routine builder's avoid edges `#ad7598` / `#af5089`: lightness only, each so its words or rails pass.
