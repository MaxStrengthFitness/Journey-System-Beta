## What was built

### Phase 1 · The shell (`91f244b`)

The seven tabs sat on a strip in two groups that needed about 1,000px in
portrait and got about 724, so Bug reports and Data started off screen. Now:

- **Places** (`src/features/admins/nav.ts`): Home · Studios · Standard ·
  Machinery. In landscape and on a desk, a sidebar lists every page under its
  place's name, two levels at most; in portrait a bar of the four places
  (each at least 88 × 44) sits at the top of the page, sticky, with the
  current place's pages as chips under it. Phase 1 had three places; Home
  arrived in phase 4.
- **One header**: the app's own. Each page's title is in the page.
- **Search** (`search.ts`, `SearchPanel.tsx`) across every studio, the machine
  catalog and everyone with an account, from what the dashboard already
  holds, so typing costs no query. Case and accents don't matter ("cirdan"
  finds Círdan), every word must match, and a name that starts with the query
  comes first. It opens at the top of the page, which stays mounted
  underneath, so a half-typed form is still there when it closes; a pick goes
  through the leave question like any other move. Ctrl K (Cmd K) opens it on
  a PC. A studio opens on its page, a person on their studio's Team, a
  machine in the catalog's own editor (`CatalogMachineHost.tsx`).
- **The kit** (`kit.tsx`): the status word, whose mark's shape carries the
  meaning as much as its colour (a filled dot is fine, a plum diamond wants a
  look, a dashed ring is "couldn't check", a hollow ring is waiting), and the
  two-line row (the whole name, which wraps; one sentence; at most one action;
  no button inside another). `admins.css` owns every `hq-` class, in the admin
  palette only; `look.test.ts` holds it (no raw colour, no ellipsis, every
  control 40px or taller). The prefix is `hq-` rather than the blueprint's
  `ad-`: ad blockers hide elements with classes like `.ad-row`.
- **Waiting for review** (Standard): the place held open for the review queue
  being built beside this room (`ReviewQueuePlaceholder.tsx`).
- Every screen that was a tab is still here, moved not rewritten; the gate is
  the one it was (`isAdmin`), refused in words for anyone else.

### Phase 2 · The Studios room (`2f5afee`)

All locations was one long page: the list, the add form, the franchises, and
the selected studio's details, team, equipment and delete panel stacked under
each other.

- **All studios** (`studios/StudiosRoom.tsx`): every studio in a group, and
  the page says what the groups are. A studio's stage (setting up, handed
  over, running) is not recorded anywhere, so the groups come from the two
  facts every studio document carries (`studios/stages.ts`): the Mindbody
  link (`registry.ts`'s `mindbodyLinkState`) and the Journey cutover date,
  read on the studio's own day. **Mindbody not set up · Runs offline · No
  cutover date yet · Moving onto Journey · On Journey**, and the practice
  studio in a group of its own. A row is the studio's whole name, its
  franchise, one sentence (the link, the cutover, its active clients, and
  since phase 3 its last pull), and a tap that opens it. A client count that
  failed says "Active clients unknown", never 0. **Add a studio** opens the
  form on request.
- **A studio's page** (`studios/StudioPage.tsx`): **Setup** (where it stands
  in two sentences; its details, the SAME form My Studio → Studio uses, so the
  two can never disagree; its franchise; the danger zone), **Mindbody** (its
  link in words, its sync since phase 3, and where its controls are),
  **Floor** (the equipment panel, the one floor editor) and **Team**
  (`studios/StudioTeam.tsx`: who works there, by `who-works-here.ts`'s one
  answer; its owners on a line of their own; temporary profiles). Switching
  tabs asks the leave question: Setup holds a form.
- **The danger zone** (`studios/DeleteStudioDialog.tsx`): deleting asks you to
  type the studio's name and lists what happens (out of every franchise
  first, then deleted; its clients, sessions and bookings stay behind with no
  studio; it can't be undone). The write is the one All locations made.
- **Franchises** (`studios/FranchisesPage.tsx`): the registry's health and the
  franchises, on a page of their own.
- The registry's writes, the add form, the franchises panel and the health
  panel moved out of `AdminStudiosTab` into their own files in
  `features/admin/studios/` (`registry-writes.ts`, `NewStudioPanel.tsx`,
  `NetworksPanel.tsx`, `RegistryHealthPanel.tsx`), unchanged but for one
  thing: the add form and the new-franchise fields now join the leave
  question. `AdminStudiosTab` and `StudioDetailPanel` are gone.

### Phase 3 · The machinery (`7d408d1`)

- **Mindbody sync** (Machinery; `machinery/`): every studio's pull at once,
  worst first (`sync-check.ts`), read from what is already stored: the studio
  document (the link, whether the automatic pull is on) and its sync lease
  (`studios/{id}/sync/lease`; the old fields on the studio are the fallback,
  as `leaseOf` does), plus Mindbody's nightly record of whether its webhook is
  on (`system/mindbodyWebhook`). The leases are read once when the dashboard
  opens (`useStudioLeases.ts`, one document per studio) and again on **Check
  again**; the ages are said as of the check. It asks Mindbody nothing and
  runs no timer. **Only failures are called failures**: a pull runs only
  while an iPad at the studio has Journey open in its hours, so a long gap is
  said as the fact it is ("No pull since Sat, Sep 26 ... either none has
  since, or the pulls stopped"). A lease that couldn't be read is **Couldn't
  check**, never "hasn't pulled". Each studio keeps its own panel on
  Operations → Mindbody (pull now, the settings, the event log); the page
  says so. The same words show on a studio's Mindbody tab and on All studios'
  rows.
- **Limbo** (`features/admin/limbo/`): grouped by the Mindbody site and
  location the events came from, each group naming the studio the registry
  says that pair is now (`limbo-groups.ts`: an exact site-and-location match;
  on a site only one studio has, that studio when it claims no location;
  never an offline studio or the practice studio). That studio starts in each
  event's picker, so the time it would land at shows at once; releasing is
  still one tap per event. **Dismiss is done at once and offers Undo**, where
  it asked first: a dismissal only stamps the event, so Undo puts it back
  exactly (`resolvedAt` back to null, the value the park wrote; the
  `dismissed` mark removed; no new field or value; `limbo-undo.ts`). A release
  has no Undo and keeps its preview. A read that failed says so with **Try
  again**, where it said "Nothing in Limbo" under the error.
- **Bug reports** (`features/admin/bugs/`): **New · Looking into it · Fixed ·
  Won't fix** (the stored values are unchanged). A new report is blue, not
  crimson: crimson is for what is critical or destructive. The count tiles
  became a sentence and status chips with their counts, which filter; a
  report's status is four buttons; an opened report shows its whole
  description. A read that failed says so, where it left every tile at zero
  and said "No reports yet".

### Phase 4 · Home (`5ffdc29`)

The dashboard opens on **what needs you**, where it opened on the list of
every location with offers, Limbo, failing syncs and bug reports each in their
own tab.

- **What needs you** (`home/needs.ts`, `home/AdminsHome.tsx`): at most seven
  items, one per kind (ten studios failing is one line), each with ONE
  condition computed from data the dashboard already holds, and each **clears
  itself** when the condition ends:
  - a studio's pulls failing **two or more times in a row** (one failed pull
    usually recovers on the next; the sync page shows every failure);
  - studios that can't pull at all (no Site ID, or no location on a shared
    site);
  - franchise listings that disagree with the studios;
  - Limbo, with which studio each group looks like;
  - machines studios offered the catalog, waiting for a decision;
  - new bug reports, with the newest quoted;
  - and **Couldn't check**, for any read that failed, with Check again as its
    door — never "nothing waiting".
  Each says a sentence, one sentence of proof, when it clears, and where to
  go. Nothing is stored about an item, so there is no Take it, Snooze or
  Dismiss (each would need a stored record).
- **The network** and **the standard**, one sentence each
  (`home/sentences.ts`): where every studio stands and how the pulls go; the
  standard set against the catalog, and what waits on corporate.
- **The reads** (`home/useHomeSignals.ts`) are ones the pages already make,
  with the same shape, once when the dashboard opens and on Check again:
  Limbo (`fetchOpenLimboEntries`), the newest hundred bug reports
  (`fetch-reports.ts`, now shared with Bug reports), and the catalog's
  pending offers (the query Machines → Submitted by studios listens to).
  Limbo and Bug reports recount when they change something. The sidebar and
  the portrait chips show Limbo's count and the new reports' count from the
  same reads.
- **Where studios set their own** (AJ's q6; `standard/`): on Machines, above
  the catalog, one line per machine where two studios or more changed the
  same **house default** on their own copy — the baseline positions, the
  body-type set-ups, a dial's default, the starting load — read from each
  studio's floor (`studios/{s}/roster`) once when the page opens. A studio's
  name for the unit and its photo are its own, not a default, and a
  switched-off machine is not counted. No badge and no mark on the catalog's
  rows (q8); nothing drawn when there is nothing to say; a floor that
  couldn't be read is named, not counted as agreeing. "Leg Press: 2 studios
  set their own seat position (Solon and Strongsville)."

### The loose ends (with this write-up)

- A tap on the sidebar or the bar while the search is open lands on the
  page, not behind the search.
- The sidebar's "Admin" label went (the app-mode switch already says where
  you are), to give the pages its room: on a landscape iPad the list should
  just fit, and where it doesn't the sidebar scrolls on its own, never under
  the bottom bar. Worth a look on the iPad.
- The Ctrl K hint shows in the sidebar (a desk) and not on the portrait bar.
- The search field takes the focus as it opens, so the iPad's keyboard comes
  up with it.

## What the screens refuse to say

- That a studio is "setting up", "handed over" or "running": nothing records
  a stage. The groups say what they are built from.
- That a sync is broken because it is quiet: only failures are failures.
- That something is empty, zero or never when it couldn't be read: the lease
  read, the client count, Limbo, the bug reports, a studio's floor and each of
  Home's reads say "couldn't" in words of their own.
- A studio's own settings as a fault: they are one quiet line, from two
  studios up.
