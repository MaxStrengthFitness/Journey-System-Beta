# My Studio — Sep 18–19 2026 (Round A of the Operations audit)

Branch `my-studio`, off `master` at `93fb096` (the fluidity round). Six
commits, one per phase, each typechecked and tested on its own, so any phase
can be reverted alone. Nothing pushed; nothing deployed. The audit it came
from is `docs/rounds/2026-09-18-operations-audit-prep.md` — sections E (the
decisions from AJ's four sittings) and F (the build plan) are the brief.

AJ's goal for the Operations dashboard, Sep 18:

> "Allow admins and owners to set standards for Max Strength by being able to
> create machines, studios and customize the default template for all studios
> to pull from — but also allowing each studio to go beyond the template and
> modify their studio to be their own. Studios should have full insight and
> control over their own studio."

And the line the whole round follows: **My Studio is where you run the
studio; Operations is where you look at it.** Round A is the first half —
the studio's own home. Round B (the Operations side: the Monday page, scope
on every tab, the Catalog's standard-set composer and the submissions queue,
Delight's row actions, the fix pile) is section F of the audit document and
has not started.

## What the audit found

A studio's own settings were in four places, none of them the studio's:
Operations → Studios (a company registry that listed every studio to every
leader, with the details form inside it), the Studio setup card under
Learning → Catalog, Relay → Team → Standards (shift hours and the deep-clean
interval) and Operations → Renewals → Settings. Letting a new hire in was
Staff & Roles, which a head trainer could not reach at all. The rules let
**any trainer edit any studio document**, including its Mindbody site id —
the hole CLAUDE.md carried since Sep 12 — while the app had no place a
head trainer could edit their own studio on purpose.

## Decisions (AJ, Sep 18, sittings 1–4)

- **Relay becomes a section of My Studio.** "Rename the Relay tab into My
  Studio, and then have a Relay section under My Studio." The bottom-bar tab
  is **My Studio**; its sections are Relay · Machines · Team · Studio. The
  view id stays `studio-tasks` (bells link to it) and the board's folder stays
  `features/planner` (thirty imports).
- **Three tiers.** The *studio tier* runs one studio: a head trainer, studio
  leader or studio owner at that studio, or a trainer with the grant. The
  *owner tier* (Owner, Franchise Owner) runs several. The *company tier*
  (Founder / Overseer, Administrator) sets the standard. "Just make all three
  have the same" — head trainer, studio leader and studio owner are one
  tier, so `StudioOwner` is labelled **Studio Owner** now, not Franchise
  Owner.
- **The grant.** "To allow studios to develop their trainers into leadership
  we need to allow leadership to be able to give trainers access to these
  menus." `Trainer.managedStudioIds` names the studios a trainer helps run;
  it opens My Studio's leader sections and every leader-only write there. It
  does not open Operations — "for now, head trainers and above have
  operations access."
- **What a studio's leaders hand out:** "trainer, head trainer, and can
  manage the studio grant — but never owner and admin." A franchise owner
  hands out the owner tier too; administrators, anything.
- **Letting people in.** "Whenever a user requests to log in they should be
  able to be granted access and additionally be linked to an existing
  Mindbody profile if available. Ideally everyone using the app will be a
  part of Mindbody except maybe some administrative." Temporary profiles are
  "something a studio can do, as well as admins."
- **Machines are adopted, never pushed.** A new studio "will have a standard
  set of machines that Max Strength recommends — usually the standard 20 —
  but the owner may adjust the standard template as they wish. Studios that
  choose to adopt that standard set can always add or remove machines from
  that." Over time a studio adopts what the standard added and removes what
  it no longer has; it can create entirely new machines or adopt
  already-created ones from the catalog. **Fields, settings and routines are
  inherited unless overridden** — the second update rule.
- **A studio can offer a machine to corporate.** "A studio leader or studio
  owner can submit a custom machine to corporate in order to be published to
  the catalog so studios can use it." The published version is adopted at
  the creating studio. Corporate decides; the machine editor stays in the
  Catalog.
- **Announcements:** "a My Studio announcements for their studio, and within
  Operations an owners-and-admins announcements only."
- **Trainers** view the floor, the standard settings and the first-time
  set-up material, leave notes on their floor's machines, and have Relay.
- **"Let's more worry about features rather than permission restrictions."**
  Sections are hidden where the rules would refuse; nothing else is fenced.

## What was built

### 1 · The shell (`src/features/my-studio/`)

`MyStudioView` owns the masthead (the studio's name, the section switch),
the section, the clock (`useNowContext`) and the two doors — the Capture
sheet and the Context Panel — through `RelayContext`, which now carries
`panel` as well as `openCapture` / `openPanel` / `closePanel`. `PlannerView`
is the **Relay** section: Floor · Mine · Notes · Network on a sub-bar under
the masthead, the Now Bar, its own frame. Its Team tab moved out to become
the **Team** section. The section is module memory (`rememberedSection`) so
an iPad reopens where it was; an arriving Planner intent (a client's
profile, a notification) always lands on Relay. `SectionFrame` gives the
other sections the same frame and Context Panel Relay draws.

`Trainer.managedStudioIds` is the grant. `leadsStudio()`
(`features/renewals/permissions.ts`) and `leadsStudioPerRules()`
(`features/learning/permissions.ts`) count it before the role check, so
`leadsHere()` — which every leader-only button already used — answers the
same as the rules. Nav label and the settings link say My Studio.

### 2 · The Studio section

The studio's own record, once, with one save bar each:

- **Details** — `StudioDetailsForm`, extracted from Operations → Studios
  (`StudioDetailPanel` renders the same component, so there is one form, not
  two). Name, time zone, accent colour, the Mindbody site and location, and
  the **Journey cutover date** (`journeyCutoverDate`, which had no UI —
  every client read as "unknown" until now). `studio-writes.ts`
  (`studioPatchPayload`) is the one writer: an empty location or cutover is a
  `deleteField()`, and the cutover must be a `yyyy-mm-dd`. **A changed
  Mindbody site id must be looked up before Save** — the lookup
  (`useMindbodyLocations`, now with an `outcome`) has to answer `ok`, so a
  typo cannot point a studio at a site that does not exist.
- **Mindbody** — the sync audit for this studio (`auditStudios`), ticking
  each minute: is the schedule arriving, when did it last, is the site
  linked.
- **The studio's day** — shift hours and the deep-clean interval, rebuilt on
  the admin kit (`useDirtyForm`). `StandardsHours` was deleted from Relay's
  Team cockpit; the Now Bar reads the same `studios/{s}.shiftHours`.
- **Renewals** — `RenewalSettingsPanel`, the same component Operations →
  Renewals → Settings renders (Round B decides whether that one goes).
- **Announcements** — this studio's notices, and the composer fixed to this
  studio (`AnnouncementComposer fixedStudioId`): the "Which studio" picker is
  hidden and the reach is never wider than the studio.

### 3 · The Machines section

The floor, and what the studio has done to it — with the Catalog page left
where it is (one implementation, two doors):

- **New in the MSF standard** — the standard machines this floor does not
  have (`floor.ts` → `standardGaps`: a switched-off machine counts as
  decided, draft and retired catalog machines and duplicate id spellings are
  left out). Adopted one at a time (`adoptCatalogMachine`), or all at once on
  an empty floor — **Adopt the MSF standard** (`seedStandardSet`, the same
  function Operations → Equipment's seed button and the inventory manager's
  "adopt the standard set" now call; it was three copies). Never pushed.
- **The floor** — `StudioInventoryManager` (search, reorder, we have this /
  we don't, out of service, a custom machine) for leaders, and the same list
  **read-only** for trainers (`readOnly` hides the toolbar, the toggles and
  the unrostered machines). Every row's **Open** button opens the machine's
  door; a row carries a flag when the machine is a submission or the
  standard dropped it.
- **The machine's door** — the Context Panel beside the list: this studio's
  standard settings (`StudioSetupCard`, the same card Learning → Catalog
  shows), the floor's notes (`StudioNotesCard`), **Local set-up**
  (`LocalSetupDialog`), **Upkeep** (`UpkeepDialog`) and, on the studio's own
  machines, **Offer to the MSF catalog**.
- **Offer to the MSF catalog** — `buildSubmission` refuses an MSF machine,
  a copy adopted from another studio and a nameless one, caps the note at
  500 characters, and writes `catalogSubmissions/{id}` (pending, as the
  signed-in person) plus a `submission: {id, status}` marker on the roster
  entry so the floor can say "waiting on corporate". Publishing is corporate's
  id migration (`scripts/migrate-machine-id.ts`) run from the PC — AJ chose
  a script over a Cloud Function — and Round B wires the queue.
- **Shared by other MSF studios** — the machine-db adoption (`planAdoption`,
  `adoptMachine`) from the floor; the All MSF machines page's messages now
  point here.

### 4 · The Team section

The Team cockpit (Relay's old Team tab: who's in today, cohorts, open
loops, the vault) on top, and **this studio's staff** underneath — what a
head trainer could not do from anywhere before:

- `useStaffRoster` is the plumbing Staff & Roles had inline (the pending
  `access_requests` stream, `POST /api/mindbody/staff`, `buildStaffRoster`);
  `StaffEditor` is the row editor. **Operations → Staff & Roles is rebuilt on
  the same two** so the Team section could not drift into a second copy.
- The tiers are three lists: `STUDIO_TIER_ROLES` (Life Transformer, Head
  Trainer, Studio Leader), `OWNER_TIER_ROLES` (+ Studio Owner, Owner),
  `ADMIN_TIER_ROLES` (+ Admin). Staff & Roles offers the owner list to a
  franchise owner and the admin list to an administrator; the Team section
  offers the studio list, with the home studio locked to this studio and the
  grant for this studio. A role out of the caller's reach is shown, disabled.
- **Letting someone in** (approve) writes `trainers/{uid}` with the role,
  the Mindbody staff link when there is one, and `managedStudioIds:
  [studio]` when "Can manage My Studio" was ticked, then marks the request
  Approved. The access-request form asks **"Which studio?"**
  (`requestedStudioId`, "Not sure yet" allowed); a request naming another
  studio is hidden from this studio's list, one naming none is shown.
- **Temporary profiles** — `ProvisionalPanel`, the same one Operations
  has, for someone not in Mindbody yet.

### 5 · The rules (`firestore.rules`, ten new tests)

- **The grant counts** in `isStudioOwnerOrHeadTrainerOnly` and
  `trainerLeads`, so it reaches every leader-only write (studio edits, team
  jobs, the vault, notices). It is an access field (`writesAccessFields`), so
  nobody grants themselves, and on update **only the studios the caller runs
  may enter or leave the list** (`grantChangeAllowed`, `ledStudios`).
- **`studios/{id}` is written by administrators, franchise owners and its
  own leaders.** Create is administrators and franchise owners. Any trainer's
  iPad may still write the schedule sync's lease — `lastScheduleSyncAt`,
  `scheduleSyncFailures` — and nothing else (`affectedKeys().hasOnly`). This
  closes the "any trainer can edit any studio" hole.
- **What an approval hands out follows the approver** (`mayHandOut`,
  `studioTierRole`, `roleChangeAllowed`): a studio's leaders mint trainers
  and studio leaders, never an owner or an administrator; a franchise owner
  never mints an administrator; a leader's grant on a new person may only
  name the studio they are joining. Anyone who runs a studio may mark an
  `access_requests` document Approved (`leadsAnyStudio`).
- **A studio's leaders post to, and take down, their own studio's notices**
  (`studioNoticeOfMine`): `targetScope == 'studio'`, one studio, never
  "all".
- **`catalogSubmissions/{id}`** — a leader creates one pending, as
  themselves, for a studio they run; the studio reads its own and may only
  withdraw a pending one (`status` and `updatedAt` only); administrators
  read, decide and delete. `catalogSubmissionValid` mirrors `buildSubmission`.

**The emulator cannot run in the cloud container**; the ten tests under
"MY STUDIO (Sep 19 2026)" in `tests/firestore.rules.test.ts` passed a syntax
check only. AJ's `npm run test:rules` is the run that counts, and the rules
deploy waits on it.

## What did not change

- The Catalog page (Learning → Catalog) and the machine editor
  (Operations → Catalog) are where they were; the Studio setup card and the
  notes card are reused through the door, not moved.
- Operations → Studios still lists every studio and renders the same details
  form; Operations → Renewals → Settings still renders the same renewal
  panel. Round B decides which of those stay.
- No Cloud Function, no Mindbody call and no existing collection's shape
  changed. The new collection is `catalogSubmissions` (AJ OK'd it, Sep 18);
  the new fields are `trainers/{id}.managedStudioIds`,
  `access_requests/{id}.requestedStudioId` and the roster entry's
  `submission` marker.
- Nothing pings anyone. A notice goes to the bell people already have.

## Traps for the next person

- **`leadsHere()` is the one answer to "does this person run this
  studio"** — a leader role at the studio, or the grant. It mirrors
  `trainerLeads` in the rules. `isStudioLeader` is role-only and will offer
  a granted trainer nothing and a visiting head trainer too much.
- **The grant is an access field.** Write it with `arrayUnion` /
  `arrayRemove` from the Team section or Staff & Roles, never from a
  trainer's own profile; the rules refuse a self-grant and any studio the
  caller does not run.
- **A studio document is written by its leaders, owners and administrators
  only.** Any new field every iPad must write (like the sync lease) goes in
  the `hasOnly` list in the `studios/{id}` update rule, or the write fails
  for trainers.
- **`studioPatchPayload` is the studio's one writer.** Both doors (My Studio
  → Studio and Operations → Studios) go through it; an empty location or
  cutover is a `deleteField()`, and the cutover is validated there.
- **`seedStandardSet` and `adoptCatalogMachine` (`admin/equipment/seed.ts`)
  are the adopt writers.** Don't add a fourth copy of the roster merge.
- **The submission marker on a roster entry is a copy.** `catalogSubmissions`
  is the truth; the marker is written after the submission (its failure only
  warns) and Round B's queue updates both.
- **Relay's tabs are four now** (`PlannerTab`): Team is a section, not a
  tab. `RelayContext` is provided by `MyStudioView`; anything that mounts
  `PlannerView` alone (a test, a future screen) has to provide it.
- **The section is module memory.** A render test that mounts `MyStudioView`
  must click back to Relay when the remembered section differs (see
  `mount()` in `planner.render.test.tsx`), or tests leak into each other.
