## The rule

`src/features/admin/journey/states.ts` is still the one rule for where a client is. The line is now:

**New · Settling in · Steady → Drifting · At risk · Lapsed (MIA) → Inactive**, with Away · Back · Unknown beside it.

- **Inactive by herself** (`automatic`): her last visit is the studio's `inactiveDays` behind her (90 by default) and nothing is booked as read. It is Lapsed's own evidence, one line further on: a client whose last visit isn't known, whose nightly record hasn't reached her or has stopped changing, or whose bookings couldn't be read is **Unknown**, never Inactive off a low Journey count (the prior-history rule). **Away is never made Inactive by itself**: a reason and a return date are not a risk.
- **Marked inactive** (`manual`): a leader's mark (`inactive.ts`): a reason from the pick list (Moved away · Injury or health · Cost · Schedule or time · Taking a break, her choice · Other), an optional note, signed with the Auth uid and dated the studio's day. It holds until she **visits after the day it was made**; a person said so, so it holds with no nightly record and over Away too.
- **One way back: a booking.** A booking makes either kind active again, and she reads **Back** (the existing state): "Booked again after 120 days away", or "Marked inactive by … (Moved away), and booked again since: she's back."
- `ClientJourney.inactive` says which kind and since when (her last visit plus the line, or the day she was marked); the night's stored state keeps `inactiveKind`.

## Phase by phase

1. **The setting** (`cff30aa9`). `inactiveDays` in `studio-settings/registry.ts`: "Inactive after … days since her last visit, with nothing booked", 30 to 730, the app's 90. It must be past the Lapsed line: `resolve.ts` (`inactivePastLapsed`) skips an Inactive that isn't (the next layer answers, Max Strength's then the app's), and when nothing beneath is past a late Lapsed the pair falls back together, as New and Settling in do. Both editors refuse a save that would break it, in words (`inactiveProblem`): My Studio → Studio → This studio's settings, and Admins → Standard → Studio defaults.
2. **The state** (`3c127df3`). `inactive.ts` (the mark, its reasons, when it holds), `states.ts` (Inactive on the line, `MIA_STATES`, `InactiveInfo`), `nightly.ts` (`inactiveKind` on the stored state; a document from before reads as the line's own), `journey-list.ts` (a page with a mark, or with a manual state whose mark is gone, works her state out itself; the Inactive list reads the most recent first). The strip has seven stops under **Starting · Settled · MIA · Inactive**; Setup → Rules names the new line; a case's rules step for an inactive client is a win-back by a person, never the app.
3. **The mark, stored** (`4d1f1831`). `studios/{s}/inactiveMarks/{clientId}`: `{ clientId, reason, note?, day, markedBy: { id: <Auth uid>, name }, markedAt }`. Its own collection: **never a whole-client write, and never the nightly job's `clientStates`**. `inactive-store.ts` is the only writer (`markInactive`, and `markActiveAgain` as a delete) and the reads (one shared listener per studio, forgotten at sign-out; one document for a single client).
4. **The nightly job** (`5b0d81d7`). See below.
5. **The screens** (`7c372e5d`). See below.
6. **The Client Directory** (`7abc0bf5`). See below.

## The rules and the index

`firestore.rules`, **the inactive round** block under `studios/{studioId}`:

- **read**: everyone who works at the studio (`wave2WorksHere`: the Directory leaves an inactive client out of All, and her profile says she is inactive);
- **create, update**: its leaders, franchise owners and administrators (`wave2Leads`), with the shape held (`hasOnly` / `hasAll`, the reason from the list, the note 1 to 300 characters when there is one, the day a `yyyy-mm-dd`, `markedBy.id` the Auth uid, `markedAt` the server's time, `clientId` the document's id);
- **delete** (Mark active again): the same leaders.

Four rules tests (leaders mark, change, read and take back; everyone who works there reads and nobody else writes, a leader of another studio is a stranger; the shape and the signature; Demo Mode's rule). `npm run test:rules`: **271 passing** on AJ's PC (the emulator stopped after).

**No new index.** Every read is a whole small collection (`inactiveMarks`, `clientStates`) or one document.

**Deploy order:** `npm run test:rules` → `firebase deploy --only firestore:rules` → push. The rules only add access, so they go first.

## The nightly job

- **Step 5, the client states** (`server/journey-step.ts`): each live studio's marks are read beside its settings and last night's states, one small collection, and handed to `nightStudio`, which writes Inactive like every other state with `inactiveKind` `"automatic"` or `"manual"`. The marks are never written by the job. A studio whose marks can't be read is **skipped** (its states stay as last night left them): a leader's Inactive is never dropped because its mark went unread. The log line counts the inactive and the marked.
- **The "always active" fix** (`features/renewals/job-plan.ts`). `pullRank` called a client active when her snapshot had a last visit or a booking ahead, and the snapshot carries her last visit forward night after night (`engine.ts`, `lastVisitHint`), so `lastVisitDate` never empties: a client who stopped coming a year ago was "active" for ever and pulled from Mindbody every month (reason 3), or as never pulled (reason 2). Now the first look reads, for each live, linked studio, last night's states (`state` and `inactiveKind` only, `select`) and its marks (`readSweepFacts`), and **`sweepsPast`** leaves an Inactive client (last night's automatic Inactive, or a mark that still holds) out of reasons 2 and 3. A sale event (reason 0) still wakes her; a **booking ahead makes her active again** and she is swept as before; the first-booking sync (`lib/first-booking-sync.ts`) is untouched. A failed read leaves everyone in the sweep, as before. The first look's log says how many were left out.

Tests: `job-plan.test.ts` (the sticky last visit, Inactive out, a sale event in; `sweepsPast` with a booking, a visit after the mark, a manual state whose mark was taken back), `nightly-first-sync.test.ts` (the whole job: left out by last night's state, by a mark, and back in with a booking), `journey-step.test.ts` (a mark written as a manual Inactive and never written itself; marks unread skip the studio), `nightly.test.ts` (both kinds stored and read back).

## Out of the way, never deleted

- **The Client Directory** (`views.ts` `inactiveHow`): All leaves out a client the Journey calls Inactive on the row's own facts (a mark that still holds unless she is booked; past the line with nothing booked on last night's record, never Away, never off what can't be judged) and a client **Mindbody itself says is inactive** (the row already called her "Inactive" since the directory round). The chip says how many; the line under it says what Inactive means; the closing line says how many are out of the way. Search finds her regardless.
- **The Hub** needed no change: it draws bookings, so an inactive client appears only when she is booked, and then she is Back.
- Nothing about her is deleted: her history, notes, packages and cases stay as they are, and the mark is a document a leader takes back.

## What is not built, and why

- **A trainer's own view of who is inactive** beyond the Directory and her profile: client states are leaders' screens.
- **The nightly job deleting an overtaken mark** (she visited after it): marks are the leaders' records; one she has visited since simply no longer holds, and the panel says so with Mark active again beside it.
- **Mindbody's own inactive flag on the Journey**: those clients still have no Journey state (`isActive !== false`, as before); the Directory counts them as Inactive.
