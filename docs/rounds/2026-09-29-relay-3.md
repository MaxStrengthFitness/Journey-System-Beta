## Built (one commit each)

1. **Claim and finish times on jobs** (`ea9ae2aa`). A join stamps `claims.{uid}` on the team job — the signed-in uid as the key, the name, the trainer id and the server's time, one map key so two iPads joining at once never collide — and stepping off removes it (`deleteField`). A leader naming someone is not a claim: a named person has no entry until they take it themselves. The line is `jobTimesLine` (`jobs/jobs.ts`): "Claimed by Sam 10:12 AM · done 10:40 AM"; "you" by either id; a finisher who never claimed it is named on the done half ("done 10:40 AM by Gil"); the time alone today, "Yesterday, 4:05 PM", "Sep 10, 9:00 AM" further back. Shown on the job sheet, the job card and the Tracker's Handed to you, Now / Coming up / Anytime and Done rows. The floor's key list in the `teamJobs` update rule gains `claims` (additive). Older jobs have no claims and say nothing new.
2. **Trainers' own cases in the Tracker's Follow-ups** (`c6d48ff7`). `useMyCases` reads `studios/{s}/cases` where `owner.id == <Auth uid>`, `outcome == 'open'`, by `dueOn` (the cases index that already exists); a failed read is unknown, never "nothing to follow up". `my-cases.ts` orders them overdue → today → by day → no day, by name inside each, and says "Overdue — was due yesterday · No next step yet — add one". A row's **Next step** opens `MyCaseEditor`: the next step, the due day, the outcome (Open · Booked again · Paused · Lost) and, for anything but Open, why; Save sends only the diff through `saveCase` (`casePatch`), stamped with the Auth uid and the server's time; typing registers with the unsaved-changes gate. The owner, the client's name and when it opened are shown, never offered. Closing it takes it off the list; the leader sees the outcome on Operations. No rule change: the wave 2 case rule already lets the owner read theirs and change those four fields.
3. **Announcements that ask "I've read it"** (`8f1979e2`; Relay q7, AJ said yes). `asksRead: true` on the notice, written only when the poster ticked "Ask everyone to say they've read it" on the composer (an older notice reads exactly as it did). On the Board's Since you were in, a notice that asks stays new — whatever the marker or a tap says — until this person taps **I've read it**: one merge write of `acks.{announcementId}: serverTimestamp()` on their own `announcementReads/{uid}`, beside the read-marks the bell already keeps there. After it lands the notice says "You said you'd read it · today at 2:12 PM" and stops asking. The retired `readBy` stamp is untouched (`docs/KNOWN-TRAPS.md`: "Never stamp readBy again"); the poster counts nobody (the record is private by rule); nobody is pinged. The `announcementReads` rule gains `acks` (a map; additive).

## Open for AJ (defaults applied)

1. **A notice that asks stays "new" on Since you were in until "I've read it", and counts in "N new"** even after Mark all read. Is that the right pressure, or should Mark all read stand in for the answer?
2. **The poster never sees who has read it.** Each person's answer is private by rule (their own `announcementReads`). If a leader should see "9 of 12 have read it", that is a new, readable record (say, `announcementAcks/{id}/{uid}`) and a rules block — not built, waiting on a yes.
3. **"I've read it" is on the Board's Since you were in only**, not in the bell's announcement sheet. Fine for the trainer between clients (the Board is where Relay asks it); say if the bell should offer it too.
4. **A leader naming someone on a job leaves no claim time.** The line says only who took it themselves; a named person who never taps "I'll take it" has no time on the job. Keep, or stamp a leader's naming as "put on it by Gil 10:05 AM"?
