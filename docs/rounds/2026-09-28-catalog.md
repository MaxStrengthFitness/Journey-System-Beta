## What was built

**Phase 1 — Find and the names** (`d8654b0`). `catalog/names.ts` holds every name a machine goes by, read from the name tables already in the code: the Academy's own movement names (the standardized setup guides' headings, pinned to `docs/msf-academy` by the test), the standard set's floor names, the Academy tab's and the makers' names, the database keys, FileMaker's grid names and the Academy's codes. A movement takes the Academy's name and a unit keeps its floor name. Only a name that means one movement may open it on its own; `names.test.ts` holds that no name opens two. `find.ts` and `CatalogFind.tsx` are the field on top of the Catalog: an exact name is the top match and Enter opens it; "lp2" is the floor's second leg press; "leg press" on a floor with two is a filter, never a guess; "handoff", "never to failure" or a maker's name filters the floor with a count; a movement the floor lacks opens in All MSF; a word inside a page lists its whole sentence and never tops. The machine's page names its movement above its floor name ("Lumb · Lumbar Extension" over LUMBAR). Learning's one search knows every name too. Less text: the index's subtitle, the Academy link cards' long lines and two of the front page's paragraphs.

**Phase 2 — Our floor** (`62cb087`). The Catalog opens on the studio's floor, numbered in the leader's walking order. A row (`FloorRow`) is the top of the machine's page: the number on the walk, the floor name whole, the Academy code, the Academy's name only where the floor name leaves it unsaid, the preset, the switches (Never to failure in crimson, Handoff plain) and the status (Out of service, Flagged). **Four floor states, never three** (`floor-index.ts`): loading, unreadable, empty and ready. The MSF standard is never drawn as the studio's floor: an empty floor says so with a door to All MSF, a failed read says "Can't read {studio}'s floor right now", and a machine link there opens in All MSF. `useStudioMachines` gains `failed`, so a failed read stops ending as an empty list. **The preset** reads the studio's own setup card first, then the unit's defaults (the Active Session's order); a dial with no number is counted ("Gap 4 · 3 not set"), and a unit with none says "No numbers set for this unit yet". No dial letters are invented. **Flags are Relay's**: the care record (`studios/{s}/machineCare`) read through Relay's own hook, never written here; flags that can't be read are "couldn't be read", never "none flagged". The Catalog's own cleaning counts and the page's Upkeep card went (question 4), and with them two reads. The page leads with the preset, says a flag whole (who, when, the note, "Cleared on My Studio → Relay.") and puts the Academy's never-to-failure rule first, in the Academy's words. Head office opens on All MSF machines. The Learning front page draws the floor only when it is ready and says only what is flagged and what is out of service.

**Phase 3 — The body and All MSF** (`85c32e9`). Three ways in above the title on every lens (`CatalogLenses`): {studio}'s floor · The body · All MSF machines, remembered until sign-out. **The body** (`BodyLens`, rules in `body-lens.ts`) is the app's own anatomy model, as AJ asked, with every part it can light also in a list beside it (Upper body · Trunk · Lower body, each with the floor's count): nothing only on the figure, nothing only on hover. A part is one of the model's regions, named for what it covers ("Upper back and lats", "Glutes and outer hip"). A part picked lists the floor's main movers, then its helpers, then the MSF movements the floor lacks, which open in All MSF; an unreadable floor never claims what it lacks. The figure is painted with Learning's `--wk-muscle-*` tokens, never the model's built-in colours. **All MSF machines** is grouped by the Academy's five families, and the Category · Kinematics · Region switch went. **Find knows the body**: "lats" or "quads" opens the body lens on that part, and a machine's own exact name still wins ("neck" opens the neck machine).

**Phase 4 — Codex R1, the source check** (`0cb9ed9`). A read-only check of every line the app holds for the twenty MSF machines against `docs/msf-academy/`: `docs/rounds/2026-09-28-codex-source-check.md`. The headline: of 795 method lines, 721 are sourced, 23 contradicted, 29 have no source and 22 are only in the setup guides; every starting weight is unsourced. The Leg Press's "Contraindicated for: Knee Replacement" is the opposite of Academy 6 (Finding 2). Never to failure is set on the right two machines and, before this round, reached a trainer only as a badge on My Studio → Machines (Finding 3). It ends in nineteen rulings for AJ, each with the Academy's answer suggested. No code, data or catalog document changed.
