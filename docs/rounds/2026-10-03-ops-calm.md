## Why it was so wordy (the causes AJ was shown)

1. **Every row explained how to clear itself.** One 45-word instruction ("Ask on the floor: if she trained…") on each of ten rows: a third of Today.
2. **Empty sections still talked.** Five of Today's eight sections were empty and each said so in a sentence; Worth a look said it four times.
3. **"Can't tell yet" on every section.** Strongsville isn't live, so there is no nightly record; that one fact was said on nearly every page, six times on Team alone.
4. **Every row carried its proof** ("Held against today. Already booked 7:00 AM this week, so not a rebook. Mindbody reported it at 10:24 AM.").
5. **The bottom line retold the page in a paragraph, and every heading had a caption** explaining what the section was.

And one that was a mistake, not a wording: **six of Today's fourteen "cancellations" were the front desk handing a Strongsville Rotation booking to Kyle at the same time.** The client was still coming.

## The rules every Operations page keeps now

- **Counts, not a written bottom line** (`CountsLine`; its rules behind an (i)).
- **Said once.** What a page can't judge yet is one note at the top (`PageNote`; the nightly record's is `brief.ts` `nightlyNote` through `useNightlyNote`: not live, no record, stale, or the clients it couldn't place). The sections it covers are neither repeated nor called clear.
- **A row is one line.** How to clear it and where it came from are on the row's (i) (`ActionRows`' Why). Long lists show five, then Show all.
- **No captions.** A heading is its name and its count; what a list leaves out is behind the section's (i) (`BriefSection` `info`) or the title's (`AdminHeader` `about`).
- **Empty folds** into one "All clear: …" line (`AllClear`), only when every read behind it answered.

A count is not a score: "Sentences, not scores" still holds, because each claim's proof is one tap away, and every minimum sample is still named (Trends folds the lines below theirs into "Not enough data yet", each a tap).

## What changed, page by page

**Changes (the rule, both Operations and the client calendar).** A cancelled booking when the client still holds another at the very same start is not a change (`changes.ts` `changesForDay`). `shortChange` is the one-line form for a row.

**Today.** The counts line; the one note; late cancels marked today as one quiet row; Needs you with the sessions nobody logged grouped per trainer (`today.ts` `notLoggedByTrainer`, `groupNames`), Late cancel inside; a visitor who can't mark gets a "Not logged" section instead; Since yesterday in short lines; Coming up says only facts that aren't zero; Worth a look only a signal that says something (Hours left it: it is on Team → Hours); All clear. `bottomLine`, `BottomLineBox` and `FreshnessLine` went. The upright tab bar no longer breaks "Today" mid-word beside its badge.

**Week.** Each page's bottom line is a counts line; the subtitle is the dates; the Trust section is a note only when a day wasn't read in full (`reviewLine` went). A day cell says only what happened beyond its count. **"Late cancel" and "cancelled late" are now two words for two things**: a leader's mark (the session taken) and a Mindbody cancellation less than a day before. Both used to read "late cancel". Changes: short rows, how the list works behind the counts' (i); `Rows`, its last reader gone, was removed.

**Month.** Counts in place of the month's sentence (`monthSentence` went); renewals and MIA stay out of the counts while the record can't judge; what a list can't show (no date of birth on file, first days still to confirm, the MIA breakdown, who nobody has talked to) behind the section's (i); a row under its day's heading no longer repeats the day.

**Month's anniversaries (AJ, during the round).** AJ, looking at "13 anniversaries wait for a confirmed first day": "even if they are not confirmed we should still show them but signify that they are not confirmed." An anniversary by Mindbody's date is now listed under its day with a quiet **Not confirmed** badge; its (i) says the date, where it came from, and to confirm or correct it on the client's Account → First day at the studio. Only Month: a milestone the app claims (Today's moments, the Hub) still waits for a confirmed day (`canClaimAnniversary`).

**Clients.** Journey: the subtitle's seven facts behind the count's (i), stops show name and count (definitions on the list's (i)), the week line is one row of counts. Renewals: no subtitle; the settings notice only when a setting is waiting, the door to My Studio in the header; an empty pipeline is one line. Moments: the paragraph above the list went (the empty list already says it). Trends: lines that met their minimum say their sentence; the rest fold into "Not enough data yet". Insights lost a developer note it showed on screen ("the redesign's Operations room, Sep 28 2026").

**Team.** This week: a counts line (on today, off today) with the page's rules behind its (i); "Their clients" left off while the record can't judge; short card lines ("41 booked · 41 not logged"). Your notes: with nothing in the Journal about a person, only the "Note for our 1:1" button (the privacy sentence once, under the (i), and on the form).
