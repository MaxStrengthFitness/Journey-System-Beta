## What was built

**Phase 1 — the engine knows the waiver, and what a card shows** (`c3e9e83`). `moments-today` gains "No waiver signed" as a Watch moment (second only to Read first; a definite "not signed" only), a moment's own words for its label, and clinical history as standing context on the entry (never a mark or a filter). `use-day-moments.ts` works the day's entries out once. `hub-schedule/card-marks.ts` decides what a card shows: the triangle's own slot, at most two glyphs in the Key's order, "+N" with its words, and only sayable words beside a glyph.

**Phase 2 — the card** (`54f0a40`). `features/hub-schedule/HubCard` replaces `components/schedule/ScheduleBlock` (deleted with its test; every promise that test held is carried over). The name she goes by, whole, wrapping; "9:30", or the span when a booking isn't the usual half hour; her number from #4 and only when it may be quoted, "New to Journey" in its place when her story began before the cutover, not repeated beside the milestone glyph; the service only when it isn't the day's usual one ("1:1 Strength Training" was on every block in AJ's screenshots). The marks are the Opportunities list's own moments; the Pulse flag and the waiver are plum; crimson is the triangle alone; the clinical dot is gone. Colour from `equipment.tokens.css` only.

**Phase 3 — the grid** (`3317e67`). `HubGrid` replaces the Hub's table. Each booking at its own start and end, 2.2px a minute (a 30-minute session 64px, a 45-minute consult 97px — the table drew a consult as an hour); overlaps side by side; an hour or more with nothing booked anywhere folded into a band that opens on a tap; your column first and pinned; the trainer row and the time axis sticky; the Now line with its time; the agreed standing weeks hatching the hours a trainer isn't on, and a day away.

**Phase 4 — the day summary** (`6c0461e`). `DayHeader`: the layers, the week with each day's count (nothing drawn for an empty day) and a dot for a day to celebrate, Today when you are elsewhere, and two small doors, Tasks (Relay) and the Key. `DaySummary`, on Schedule: the day in words and the Opportunities list's own five chips; a chip lights its cards and dims the rest (the spotlight), and the bar says what it shows ("Showing 3 to celebrate: 2 birthdays, 1 milestone"), steps to the next card, opens the same group as a list, or is done. `KeySheet`: every mark and state in words.

**Phase 5 — the peek** (`2f5dbdb`). A tap on a card: her name, time, trainer and number; Read first, whole; every other mark as its sentence; last in and her package with "can't tell" rather than a guess; clinical history on file, quietly; Open profile and Start session. Beside the card on an iPad on its side, centred when upright.

**The Hub, mounted** (`6ad712e`). The screen had no mounted test. `components/ClientsView.render.test.tsx` mounts the real Hub and proves the columns, the cards, the consult's length, the staff time, the hatching, the week, the chips, the peek, Start session, the spotlight and the lazy Opportunities layer. It passes under Eastern, UTC and Pacific time.

**Phase 6 — this document**, the two READMEs, the checklist's Round 25, CLAUDE.md, ARCHITECTURE's screen map and decision log, KNOWN-TRAPS, the roadmap, and the colour ratchet lowered from 231 to 219 (the Hub's raw colours are gone).

## Open, for AJ

1. **The tap.** A tap on a card now opens the peek (question 1's default). The profile is two taps away (card, Open profile); so is a session (card, Start session). Keep it?
2. **All stars** (question 6): a proposed rule to confirm — twice a week on average, in at least 9 of every 10 weeks, for the last 26 weeks. It needs a client's weekly attendance, which Journey holds in full only after the migration.
3. **The hatching** shows only for trainers with an AGREED standing week (My Studio → Team). Until leaders agree the weeks, the grid shades nothing — honest, but it means the Keep ("who's working, at a glance") arrives as the weeks are agreed.

5. **The briefing** still marks every 25th session and "back after 21 days" (`lib/hub-markers.ts`). Move it onto the one engine next?
6. **The top in portrait** is three rows (the layers and doors, the week, the chips). Calm enough?
