# Journey on the floor: the one page

*For trainers at launch (Oct 10 2026). Print it, or read it to the team at the
first huddle. Everything here is how the app already works.*

## Getting in

- **Open Journey from the J icon on the iPad's Home Screen**, and sign in
  inside it. The icon keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.
- **Sign in as yourself**: Google, or Microsoft with your
  @maxstrengthfitness.com address. The first time, ask for your studio; a
  leader lets you in on My Studio → Team.
- **Pin the studio once per iPad** in the studio picker. After that, whoever
  signs in on that iPad is greeted with it.
- **Handing the iPad over? Tap "Not you? Sign out".** The next person starts
  clean. If your session is still open, it asks first.
- **Practise in Demo Mode** (in the studio picker), never on a real client.

## Every session is logged in Journey

From your studio's first day on Journey, a booking with no session logged
shows up on Operations → Today as "not logged". A late cancel is marked on the
booking by anyone (the Hub's peek: Late cancel · session taken); a no-show is
a leader's mark on Operations → Today. Neither counts as a visit.

## A client's first session in Journey

- **Their total so far comes from Mindbody.** On Notes & Profile → Account,
  check "About N before Journey (from Mindbody)" and tap Confirm, or Change it.
  Do the same for First day at the studio. About 20 seconds, once per client.
- Until it's confirmed, the app won't celebrate a milestone or an anniversary.

## When something is off

- **The Wi-Fi drops:** keep logging. Sets save on the iPad and send by
  themselves when it's back; the line under the session bar says what's still
  sending, and Finish never hangs. Don't close or reload Journey while offline.
- **The client isn't in Journey yet, or Mindbody is down:** Client Directory →
  **Open session**. Run it as usual and choose the client with **Who's this?**
  at any time, or at Finish. Settings you type go to the client then.
- **A brand-new person Mindbody doesn't have yet:** New client makes a
  temporary profile. A leader joins it to the real record once Mindbody has
  them (My Studio → Team), and the sessions move with it.
- **Someone else's session is open on another iPad:** you can watch it, or
  take it over. A session belongs to the trainer running it.
- **"A new version is ready":** it loads by itself only on the Hub, never in a
  session. Anywhere else, tap Load now when you're free.

## Once per iPad

Settings → Accessibility → Touch → **Shake to Undo: off** (otherwise iOS
offers to undo what you typed when the iPad is carried across the floor).
